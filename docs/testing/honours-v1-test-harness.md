### Wonder Crown owner correction — 31 October

Wonder ranks by **(number of verified Wonder victories, accumulated Wonder points)**, in that order. The former *fastest Wonder win ≤60m with 7d maturity* was a worker mistake and is **superseded**. One accepted Wonder victory is enough to activate and claim the current Wonder trophy; only a player with one or more **own** Wonder victories can hold it. A verified completed Wonder scores **0.5**, a personally credited Wonder victory **2**, a verified allied player's Wonder victory **1**, and winning an officially sanctioned Wonder Race challenge **3 additional** points. An approved Race win that is also a Wonder victory credits 2+3 and, if the winning Wonder completion belongs to that player and is verified, +0.5. These are the owner's actual point values, not provisional calibration numbers.

In the test engine, points are represented as **integer half-point units**. Wins are primary; a player with 2 wins/5 points outranks one with 1 win/5.5 points. If victory counts tie, more points transfers possession. An exact equality preserves the existing holder; simultaneous first exact ties remain pending instead of choosing upload order. Source corrections recompute count, points and legitimate dated succession. **Acceptance timestamps are only used for factual holder succession, never as an eligibility window or fastest-result criterion.** Building points require reviewed completed-building identities; team ally points require official co-winning teammates and FFA ally points require source-verified effective alliance at Wonder victory. A source-verified challenge result cannot replace the base Wonder victory.

### Source envelope and privacy boundary

Inputs are normalized, *trusted-only* facts, with Season ID, canonical Battle ID, canonical Game ID, accepted source and result revisions, comparison cohort, format/roster, Game clock, outcome/official victory method, and source-observed metric evidence. The test evaluator does not independently authenticate these facts. The new pure test-only `honoursV1StatisticsAdapter.mjs` maps actual AoF canonical Match formats (ONE_V_ONE, TWO_V_TWO etc.), source-current GameStatistics, server-accepted GameOutcome and explicitly verified additional per-metric admissions to the sandbox input, refusing stale hashes, mismatched Game/Match/Season/roster/result revisions and unreviewed Wonder methods. This is an **adapter for trusted emulator snapshots**, not a Firestore reader or a proof producer. It cannot make missing Explorer, 30m Boom/Gold or Wonder-method witnesses appear. **It MUST NOT be used as an externally callable function over raw client-supplied evidence.** A future versioned admission adapter must derive them from the existing source-current READY replay, accepted canonical official result, authenticated mapping and approved model revisions. Missing or incompatible evidence yields `PENDING_EVIDENCE`, not guessed completion.

Chronicle-dependent Aggression/Defence cases output **candidate drafts**, not actual invitations; a real offer still requires the approved Chronicle source-opportunity review, private authenticated actor lookup, strict one-respondent/one-Battle invitation, **three intervening eligible Battles** cooldown, version fences and privacy. The test planner reserves cooldown across *the proposed batch as well as prior offers*, and excludes unauthorised/unknown Battle IDs. In `honoursV1ChronicleSandbox.mjs`, a respondent can simulate `ACKNOWLEDGED`, `NOT_PERSONALLY_MEANINGFUL`, `UNSURE` or `DECLINE` only when `FUNCTIONS_EMULATOR=true`, project begins `demo-` and `AOF_HONOUR_CHRONICLE_V1=emulator-only`. The simulator verifies exact authenticated respondent and source revision; it never stores or sends the invitation, never publishes subjective testimony, never writes social credits or grants an honour. A source correction changes revision and invalidates old recognition. One player’s judgement cannot invent a reciprocal action, actual combat effects, gold receipts, map fog, Wonder-victory method, or a public accusation.
