## 1. Product intention, hard boundaries and shortlist

These are **exceptional historical recognitions**, not six new scoring tracks. They confer no numeric bonus, instant stage, competitive benefit, inferred mutuality or automatic trophy. A rare event should mark the latest chapter of an independently established story, rather than celebrate one large raw command count. The social tracks stay independent: Gallantry, Cruelty and Chivalry belong to **one player**; Rivalry, Hostility and Bond are independently earned in **A→B / B→A** directions. One underlying scene may inform more than one *separately qualified* track without duplicating deeds.

| Track | Recommended event | Memory hook / what is truly new | Evidence-readiness at audited baseline | Launch classification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gallantry | **Against the Tide** | A personally initiated substantial offensive challenge in an unfavourable, known pre-Battle competitive matchup | Rating/history + directed raids/contests exist; offense admission needs #122/#126 | Conditional candidate; feasible with conservative inference |
| Cruelty | **The Iron Harvest — Returning to the Fields (V1 variant)** | Repeat, independent pair-economic pressure across distinct league Battles and renewed current-Battle economic episodes; **not proof of weakness** | Raid V3, episode source lineage and pair Battle history are useful candidates; qualification needs #126 + owner rule | **Default-off inference proposal**; original weakened-opponent strict subtype held — see v1.1 companion |
| Chivalry | **The Shield at the Gate** | Helper-specific defensive intervention during an attributable threat to another player's home | Engagement V4 and pair episode context exist; recipient benefit is inferred, not proven rescue | Conditional candidate; conservative inference gate |
| Rivalry | **The Reckoning** | A real two-sided rematch following a documented series and a verifiable reversal | Accepted pair results and pair encounters available; each participant's conduct must qualify independently | Strongest initial implementation prospect |

| Bond | **Stand Together** | Beneficiary-specific help is returned in another independent Battle; mutuality is earned action by action | Fixed-team assistance evidence exists, but materiality/FFA attribution conditional | Conditional candidate; conservative inference gate |

**Rarity is qualitative, not a hidden percentile.** All six require a conjunctive rare situation (qualified current deed + historical context + source authority + policy). We do not invent numeric ratios, rating gaps, Battle-count minimums, interaction frequency, materiality weights or rate limits. Corpus calibration and the owner's explicit rule approval determine any numbers. A category may be in the catalogue yet **unavailable** for automated recognition where its required witnesses cannot be established. It is better to launch with fewer awarded exceptional recognitions than manufacture six.

### Common non-negotiable recognition contract

1. **Current evidence:** eligible human league player(s), active authoritative READY recording and accepted Game/source/identity binding, canonical Game→league Battle→Event, ordered source operation(s), versioned raw observation and analysis model, independent source-deed lineage and explicit coverage. Non-scoring eligible warmups count; duplicate POV uploads and Game retries do not create new Battles.
2. **History:** resolve prior pair standing, prior independently qualified episodes, results and/or pre-Battle rating as of the **actual event time**, never current standing or upload order. One Game's consequences cannot be used to prove that same episode's entry difficulty or victim weakness. Cross-Game order is authoritative or dependent recognition waits.
3. **Inference:** name separately (a) recorded source fact, (b) model-bounded conduct inference, (c) historical significance classification and (d) optional player interpretation. Inference-first ordinary launch is owner-directed in #122, but the precise deviation from strict REL-V1-EVID-01 is **not yet a ratified numeric/evidence policy**. Until its gate and adversarial tests pass, retain as candidate, do not silently award.
4. **Independent effects:** recognition alone changes **no** track. If the underlying source incident also qualifies ordinary effects under separately approved rules, emit independent receipts. No new magnitudes or accelerated stages. Level 3+ ordinary Relationship reciprocity must have independently qualifying opposite-direction historical deeds. Only Treachery has the already-approved exception.
5. **Negatives/uncertainty:** unknown target ownership, competing explanations, missing source range, partial territory/diplomacy history, a merely issued ORDER, passive recipient, ambiguous prior standing or unresolved incident independence blocks the affected claim. Do not infer absence from missing telemetry. Mark PENDING_EVIDENCE versus NOT_QUALIFIED versus UNCONFIGURED_POLICY distinctly.
6. **Idempotence:** reuse neutral incident/sourceDeedLineage as the anchor; one selected recognition for an independently resolved historical turning point. Merge facets of the same pressure campaign and multiple Games into **one pair×Battle Chronicle chapter**. Retain all raw evidence even when presentation selects only the strongest meaningful beat. Corrections replace/retract/re-evaluate both recognition and dependent history; never accumulate old and new award copies.
7. **Editorial tone:** historical, restrained, authored, neither comic badges nor courtroom accusations. No proved killing, damage, successful Castle, rescue, defencelessness, motive or hostility of feeling without specific proof. Event names are editorial metaphors; visible prose states the limited evidence.

## 2. Selected event specifications

The examples below are **hypothetical placeholders**, not assertions about actual players or matches. A/B/C are roster-mapped players. Each exemplar must bind the eventual factual verbs to an admitted witness type.

### E-GAL-01 — Against the Tide (Gallantry)

**Promise.** A repeatedly tested contender takes the initiative against a demonstrably formidable opponent, and still puts meaningful, attributable offensive pressure into the match. Outcome can be a loss: daring is not winning.

**Eligibility / historical rarity.** A has reliable pre-Battle competitive context against B: an approved pinned rating-based mismatch **or** a more specific historically qualified pair record, plus an earlier meaningful shared competitive chapter. The relevant comparator and confidence are known before the new Battle; the qualifying incident happens after A has a genuine opportunity to contest B and A undertakes a substantive, pair-directed offensive episode. In team games, overall team Elo disparity is not an A-versus-B strength measurement; pin actual opponent identity and accessible engagement. Do **not** assert inferior on-map military material from a rating gap. Use an additional genuinely verified incident-entry force mismatch only if available in future.

**Evidence.** Source-deed-linked Raid V3 / Skirmish V2 / Engagement V4 attacker→B participation; actor-specific sufficient source variety and episode independence; accepted pre-Battle rating/snapshot plus prior pair history; independently qualified bounded offense semantics from #122/#126. Later win, later economy or generic aggressive personality is corroboration at most. Opponent strength and offense **both** required.

**False positives.** One high-volume attack order, an outmatched **team** but actually favoured A, an uncontested opponent absent from A's feasible target set, provisional rating, empty opening rush, repetition of one micro-episode, later score deficit recast as pre-entry adversity, and an upset win without qualifying offense.

**Rarity / effects.** Rare historical mismatch + substantiated initiative. Possible **A's player-owned Gallantry** ordinary strengthening only when its distinct track rule qualifies; Rivalry A→B separately if the pair contest qualifies. **No automatic Cruelty/Hostility effect or weakening**. Not a mutual Rivalry credit for B. No exceptional stage change.

**Question.** None. A's daring must not require B to praise or confirm A. **Honours hook:** proposed Chronicle personal-leaf emblem of a battered standard; #129 may decide whether a later, source-qualifiable collectible/title exists, but none is granted now.

**Example scenes**
- *An Unfavourable Standard*: "The ledger had B ahead of A before this meeting. A nevertheless directed a sustained offensive episode toward B; the outcome did not decide whether the challenge was made."
- *The Long Odds Returned*: "Their earlier encounters had already favoured B. On meeting again, A took the initiative in an independently recorded contest. The Chronicle names the challenge, not an imaginary battlefield victory."

**Alternatives rejected for V1:** **The Outnumbered Charge** (requires actual force-count state at entry, not rating), **The Crown Defied** (an upset win is a result/achievement, not Gallantry by itself). Both may be later honours after stronger proof.

### E-CRU-01 — The Iron Harvest (Cruelty) — strict future subtype (original design)

**The original strict subtype below is preserved, not the V1 interpretation; see the linked v1.1 companion for Returning to the Fields.** A directs substantial renewed pressure at B **after B is already shown to be materially disadvantaged**. The unusual order of events is the story: an established weakness preceded a separately independent assault. Not a moral judgment of A.

**Eligibility / historical rarity.** A current independent directed economic-pressure or military-pressure episode against B; independently qualified, **sustained pre-existing** B disadvantage at that new episode's entry; relevant material target attribution and causal non-overlap. Context should show why this was punishing pressure, not simply an evenly contested raid. Prior pair encounters may enrich the Chronicle but cannot substitute for evidence of the disadvantage. A's initial episode that created weakness does not retroactively become Iron Harvest.

**Evidence.** Raid V3, Engagement/target-controller/accepted context and new independently supported H2-style pre-entry score/material trajectory with sufficient timeline continuity and comparability. Historical league standing or pregame Elo **is not** proof that B was currently losing economically. Neither Gold Control's infrastructure influence nor unverified villager deaths is a substitute. Materiality and disadvantage tests require owner-calibrated rules.

**False positives.** A single raid; repeated commands in one raid; ordinary focus fire; B's deficit visible only after A's act; victory margin or lower rating used as a substitute for contemporary weakness; misidentified civilian structure; tactical defense against B disguised as punishing offense. If the pre-entry weakness witness is unavailable, no event even when raid commands look extreme.

**Rarity / effects.** Strictly rare; **currently evidence-blocked** rather than a pretext for arbitrary Cruelty. Possible **A's Cruelty** ordinary strengthening only if separately admitted. May also independently weaken **A→B Bond** if B was an established beneficiary of prior A cooperation and a separately qualified serious-antagonism rule applies; **not B→A by reflex**, and ordinary Rivalry/Hostility do not automatically follow. No stage jump.

**Question.** None: do not ask the victim to validate a tactical or moral accusation. **Honours hook:** blackened sickle engraving as an unawarded event illustration; #129 decides whether Cruelty-themed honours are appropriate, never a reward for unverifiable civilian harm.

**Example scenes**
- *After the Balance Had Shifted*: "The qualified account places B at a sustained disadvantage before A's next independent offensive episode. A continued directed pressure. The record does not tell us what A believed or intended."
- *Pressure Upon Pressure*: "One encounter had already left B behind by the admitted measure. In the next separately established engagement, A again pressed toward B's economy. No unverified destruction is entered in the book."

**Alternatives:** **Ashes at the Granary** (cooperative-history + independently qualifying economic breach; may be more feasible later but duplicates rupture themes and needs target/command gates); **Merciless Pursuit** (chasing or wiping fleeing units requires movement/outcome execution witnesses currently absent). *The Iron Harvest* remains **deferred for automatic launch** unless H2 is proved; do not weaken the gates merely to populate Cruelty.

### E-CHI-01 — The Shield at the Gate (Chivalry)

**Promise.** A intervenes in a threatening engagement directed at B's home area, with A's own eligible defensive participation and B as an identifiable beneficiary. The Chronicle honours attempted protection; it never invents that a teammate was "saved."

**Eligibility / historical rarity.** There is a qualified C→B threat anchored to B's home/starting Town Center geometry; A independently contributes to the same defensible military encounter or a closely source-linked directed defensive action; A→B beneficiary attribution is unambiguous. A normal shared army, nearby garrison or "reinforcement" issued without admitted beneficiary-specific effect is insufficient. Existing pair history (e.g., prior antagonism or previous B→A help) makes the *turning point* rarer and more evocative but does not replace protective conduct.

**Evidence.** Engagement V4 defensive-assistance provenance + Pair Episode Context V1 DEFENSIVE_SUPPORT_WITH_PRESSURE, exact source Skirmish/attacker→defender local contest, stable A/B/C identities and a reliable home anchor; #124 context and #126's reviewed bounded meaningful-help inference. Distinguish claimed spatial inference from confirmed protection. A third-party threat must be correctly ascribed; no invented destroyed attacking units, aid receipt or averted defeat.

**False positives.** A is merely B's teammate; A commanded units near B but targeted unrelated C; B's own actions copied to A; 4v4 shared outcome; unresolved geography; threat was actually against A; inferred rescue from a surviving TC; two overlapping detector windows treated as two acts.

**Rarity / effects.** Rare context-dependent *noteworthy* assistance. Potential **A's Chivalry** ordinary strengthening and independently **A→B Bond** strengthening on separate qualifications. Qualifying help by A may independently cool **A→B Hostility**, never B→A simply because B received it. No Rivalry erasure, no stage jump.

**Optional Chronicle question** (only if #128 ranks it above other offers for B in this Battle): to B, **"Did A's intervention in this recorded defense register as meaningful support to you?"** Structured: meaningful support / ordinary teamwork / cannot say. These are recipient perspective, not proof of rescue. **Honours hook:** a small shield on the pair chapter, not an automatic trophy.

**Example scenes**
- *Under the Home Banner*: "C's pressure was recorded near B's home ground. A's independent commands entered the same qualified defensive encounter. Whether the intervention turned the battle is not established."
- *An Old Opponent Beside the Gate*: "Earlier chapters had placed A and B on opposing sides. This time A's source-linked participation stood on B's side of a home defense—an unexpected change in the recorded account, not a declaration of friendship."

**Alternatives:** **The Last Garrison** (asserts that A saved B's last TC; outcome proof missing); **The Gift in Winter** (substantial tribute under threat; actual recipient receipt is not proved by DE_TRIBUTE request). Both remain future proof-gated variants.

### E-RIV-01 — The Reckoning (Rivalry)

**Promise.** A fresh, source-linked contest changes the texture of a documented head-to-head history: the familiar adversaries meet again, **both independently contend**, and a reliable result/contest reversal makes this encounter a plausible chapter title.

**Eligibility / historical rarity.** Prior independent pair-specific contest history in different league Battles, not team co-presence. A present Battle supplies qualified A→B competition **and**, to describe a two-sided encounter, separately qualified B→A offensive participation or substantive defensive resistance (not mere victimhood). To call it *the Reckoning*, a verifiable historical turn also exists: a reversed accepted pair result in a genuine 1v1, or an owner-approved pair-specific reversal supported by the same source context. A 4v4 team win does not establish that A personally overcame B. Without the reversal, normal Rivalry deed(s) remain but **not this exceptional event**.

**Evidence.** Canonical official Game/Match outcome revisions for 1v1 result arc; past and current qualified Pair Social Evidence V1/local direct contest and RETURN_PRESSURE where genuinely independent; full historical Game ordering; active responder source for each direction. Score margin, moving to an attack and survival alone are not reciprocal action proof.

**False positives.** Scheduled rematches without actual pair engagement; a passive B granting reciprocity; a new roster co-player in a team Game; a changed current skill level read backward; apparent turnaround from a corrected/stale result; copied multi-POV, duplicated raid fragments or incomplete pair history.

**Rarity / effects.** Very rare relative to ordinary contests: prior qualified history + independently active encounter + genuine reversal. Each direction receives its *own* possible ordinary Rivalry qualification; the recognition itself grants nothing. No automatic mutual stage, no automatic Hostility, Bond weakening or Rivalry protected-floor removal. The stage-3 floor continues to require the approved explicit skill-superiority acknowledgment before its regression gate can open.

**Question.** Normally none. If the independently existing **third distinct loss** superior-skill opportunity qualifies, #128 may choose its approved question for the **loser**, not an invented "Was this a rivalry?" prompt. **Honours hook:** crossed banners in the shared chapter; no title without #129 approval.

**Example scenes**
- *The Ledger Turns*: "Across earlier qualified meetings B had prevailed. This time A's contest with B was again substantive, and the accepted 1v1 result went the other way. Their record now bears a reversal; no motive is attributed to either player."
- *Answer and Counteranswer*: "The two had fought directly before. On this occasion A pressed and B supplied independently qualified resistance in the same engagement. A subsequent accepted result altered the course of their head-to-head account."

**Alternatives:** **The Unbroken Duel** (marathon rematches risk rewarding fixture repetition without distinction); **The Grudge Match** (implies Hostility from ordinary competition and player emotions). *The Reckoning* protects competitive drama without inventing resentment.

### E-HOS-01 — The Broken Truce (Hostility) — strict future subtype (original design)

**The strict effective-rupture candidate below is preserved, not the V1 alternative; see the linked v1.1 companion for E-HOS-02 Old Wounds Reopened.** A real cooperative phase ends at A's independently initiated effective rupture, followed by A's separately qualified directed antagonism toward former ally B. A meaningful breach *without* the confirmed king-loss / victim-betrayed Treachery exception.

**Eligibility / historical rarity.** Complete, qualified prior **mutual effective** alliance at incident entry, relevant historical cooperative fact (or previously established supported Bond), an unambiguous **effective** rupture initiated by A rather than response to B, and independently admitted A→B meaningful antagonistic conduct *after* the rupture. Merely declaring Enemy or attacking an ordinary scheduled opponent does not qualify. No "unprovoked" or trust/motive claim. Strict objective Treachery candidate is higher priority and must not be preempted by a duplicate historic accolade for the same king-loss rupture.

**Evidence.** Effective temporal directed diplomacy both A→B and B→A, Game mode eligibility, exact full-moment ordering, pair assistance history and a source-linked distinct A→B offensive incident with qualified target/circumstances; independently approved **ordinary** distinct-Hostility predicate from #122/#126. Current decoded diplomacy commands are **COMMAND_ONLY** in the documented foundation; these cannot prove effective alliance/rupture. This candidate therefore remains held until the effective-diplomacy qualification gate is satisfied.

**False positives.** One-sided Ally declaration; unknown current initial diplomacy; switch requested but not effective; A responding to an earlier B breach; combat before rupture; ordinary allied status in a fixed-team Game; generic 1v1 war; A's offensive toward C; assumed economic harm from a click; qualified Treachery being double-labelled as a second exceptional stage rule.

**Rarity / effects.** Severe, but categorically below Treachery. Potential A→B **ordinary Hostility** strengthening only under a specific approved predicate. May independently weaken existing **A→B Bond** if this is qualified serious antagonism. Do not synthetically award B→A Hostility or require B's statement to prove objective rupture. **No stage jump, no Treachery victim binding, no exception to reciprocity.**

**Optional Chronicle question** (only if admitted qualified antagonistic opportunity ranks highest for B): to B, **"Did A's recorded action after the alliance ended leave a lasting grievance for you, or did you regard it as ordinary competition?"** Structured: personal grievance / ordinary competition / no lasting change / undecided. If the objective **Treachery** opportunity qualifies, its victim-only *betrayed/fair game* choice has priority; this lesser prompt must be suppressed, not used to bypass it. **Honours hook:** parted banner, an event illustration only.

**Example scenes**
- *Banners Divided*: "A and B had been in a qualified mutual alliance. A initiated the recorded effective rupture and later conducted a separate directed offensive episode toward B. The record does not establish why the alliance ended."
- *An Alliance in the Past Tense*: "Earlier support between these players is part of their history. In this Battle, a qualified alliance ended at A's initiative before the next admitted antagonistic action toward B. This is not the king-loss Treachery judgment."

**Alternatives:** **Old Wounds Reopened** (former Bond, later opposed Battle and extraordinary directed antagonism; needs an independently approved Hostility distinctness rule even without diplomacy), **The Marked Enemy** (focused targeting; multiple *accessible* targets and comparable opportunity not proven in existing V1 statistics). Neither makes ordinary war automatically hostile.

### E-BON-01 — Stand Together (Bond)

**Promise.** Across separate recorded league Battles, help ceases to be a one-way incident: A meaningfully helps B; in another independently sourced episode B meaningfully helps A. The newest assistance gives the long relationship a recognisable return chapter.

**Eligibility / historical rarity.** Source-qualified, beneficiary-specific substantive or bounded-inferred meaningful assistance in **both real directions**, anchored to independent Battles with reliable history and active identities. At least one noteworthy aid episode is connected to an actual pressure context or material need. This is not merely two simultaneous players who attacked C, nor simply being assigned the same team twice. Aid can occur in FFA if recipient and effect are independently supported; formal alliance is not mandatory.

**Evidence.** Pair social assistance + Engagement V4 beneficiary-linked defensive participation and pair context; independently qualified aid route for each direction and moment; original Battle/episode IDs and chronology. Tribute requested ≠ receipt. Fixed-team cooperation provides stronger available source context, but FFA is not automatically ineligible or automatically qualified.

**False positives.** B merely survived A's support episode, same roster across seasons, A/B shared target without beneficiary-specific effect, mirror credit granted from receipt, two detector facets of one command, different recipients in group assistance, fake chronology from upload order, donations recycled within an unqualified incident.

**Rarity / effects.** Rare reciprocal directed history, not a special automatic stage. A→B Bond strengthens only with A's independently qualified contribution; B→A only with B's. Qualified A→B aid may separately cool **A→B Hostility**, and B→A aid may cool **B→A Hostility**, never the other direction by passive receipt. No automatic erasure of historical rivalry/peaks and no forced "mutual level 3." No reputation transfer; Chivalry independently requires noteworthy protective context.

**Optional Chronicle question.** Normally none; the record is enough. If the newest specific aid makes #128's existing **meaningful-assistance** opportunity especially worthy, the eligible **recipient** may receive that single approved support-perspective question, not both players by default. **Honours hook:** interlaced shields or paired standards; perhaps a prestigious long-lived cooperative title for #129 review, but never an automatic grant from this proposal.

**Example scenes**
- *The Help Returned*: "In an earlier Battle A contributed source-linked help to B. A later Battle contains separate B→A assistance. Their record now shows help in both directions, each attached to its own deed."
- *No Single Banner Required*: "Their cooperation was not established by a common team assignment alone. Two independently sourced incidents in different encounters identify each player as a helper to the other. Whether the players call that friendship remains theirs to say."

**Alternatives:** **Brothers in Arms** (mere repeated common-team victories mistakenly imply mutual help); **The Unexpected Hand** (a one-time FFA rescue may be distinctive but actual beneficiary/impact evidence is weak and "rescue" presumes outcome). *Stand Together* has the strongest longitudinal story.

## 3. Treachery non-duplication and exceptional chronology

**Existing Treachery is unchanged** and keeps the *sole* approved stage exception. Required: mutual effective alliance, qualified unilateral rupture, actor's post-rupture hostile participation, independently proven victim-king loss and responsible former ally, association/mode/order/identity conditions, and **victim's explicit binding "betrayed" judgment**. Only then victim→responsible former ally Hostility is raised to 3 if below, else +1 to configured maximum. It supplies **no mutual reciprocity** or mutual Feud and remains correction-sensitive. Fair-game/unanswered/unsupported victim response cannot trigger it. This catalogue neither relaxes source proof nor introduces another king-loss event or automatic status effect.

For one incident with a potential Treachery and Broken Truce: keep the underlying neutral rupture facts, preserve an unresolved Treachery candidate privately where required, choose **one** resolved exceptional recognition for the historic incident, and prefer Treachery after complete qualification. Do not double-consume ordinary units, surface private pending victim testimony, or pretend that an unqualified Treachery is proved. Independent older/nontreachery deeds keep their own ordinary eligibility under #127.
