## Comparability: ordinary map/civ variation is NORMAL, unusual starts are SPECIAL

Do **not** gate Fast Castle against an **identical map ID** or **identical civilization ID**. Ordinary maps/civs may enter one *standard-start competitive class* if the metric-specific starting opportunity, initial age, starting TC and resources, clock/game speed, patch/catalog model and supported source are genuinely comparable. Explicitly include **civilization effects and legitimate bonuses as normal AoE2 competitive expression** where they do not change the measurement or create a demonstrably different start; show civ/map in record evidence. Standard 1v1 and fixed teams can be evaluated under the same basic clock-start opportunity if genuinely equivalent, while preserving format labels and a drill-down. FFA may qualify when its opportunity is actually the same; this is not a blanket FFA exclusion or automatic inter-format equivalence.

**Separate or withhold direct timing comparisons** when there is a known materially different opportunity: **Nomad, Migration, custom/scenario starts, altered starting age/resources/TCs, unusually configured fish/nomadic/water economies, game-speed or data-mod differences, unqualified civ/model mechanics, or missing start proof**. An ordinary hybrid or water map is not automatically incomparable: test whether water economy materially alters this specific record, rather than assuming map ID determines the result. Dedicated **qualified cohort records** can still be displayed with their actual scope, but a special-start winner is never silently crowned the universal fastest standard-start champion.

Apply this same **opportunity-not-identity** reasoning to all 26 Record Book candidates by measurement family:
- **Time to action / age**: compatible starting opportunities and clock needed; ordinary map/civ IDs generally not a filter.
- **Checkpoint at 20/30m**: original source must span cutoff; civ/start/resource availability and metric model valid. Missing checkpoint ≠ zero.
- **Whole-Game maxima (farms, raids, buildings, economy zones, relic touches)**: raw record is historical **volume/exposure**, not demonstrated skill; disclose Game duration/opportunities. Do not silently compare a 2h FFA to a 15m 1v1 as equal proof of skill; use separate format/duration classes or a clearly qualified normalized-rate companion, not fabricated normalized raw values.
- **Rates/APM**: exact qualifying observed Game length, minimum credible duration, same action-class/model version; one short opening burst cannot become an all-game APM record. Show raw APM, not skill.
- **Social/team assistance**: helper→beneficiary→attacker and fixed team at incident; normalize against genuinely threatened-ally opportunities for any skill claim. No proof of an actual rescue.
- **Win streak / fastest victory**: final official outcome, correct Game vs Battle unit and season match chronology; BO3 Game wins must not become a 3-Battle streak; handle walkovers and unusual victory formats separately.
- **Mixed evidence model versions**: partition or explicitly version-compatible transform; never average incompatible models and call it one exact result.

### Smallest truthful possession model

The trophy's **scope is the chosen approved Season and comparable standard-start opportunity cohort**, not automatically an all-Season League title. If a future owner chooses league-wide rolling scope, that is a different explicitly approved title/policy, not a silent extension. Keep **one active trophy identity per approved Season×record-cohort**, with an optional succession log of legitimate holder intervals.

The baseline Record Book can rank current comparable single-Game timings even when the Season is sparse. **Maturity is an award-activation gate, not a ban on recording early performances or a requirement to wait until Season end.** The owner-approved maturity policy must include meaningful elapsed official competition **AND** independent sufficiently representative comparable player/Battle participation; its numbers (`D_elapsed`, `N_comparablePlayers`, `N_distinctEligibleBattles`) and any independent absolute excellence limit (`T_FC`) remain **null, unapproved**.

1. **Before maturity:** show a source-qualified *provisional record* with its actual comparison scope, but trophy possession is **unclaimed / pending maturity**, even if this is the only result. A player's first genuinely qualified FC achievement and private PB are not delayed.
2. **At first approved maturity crossing:** recompute the complete **current accepted comparable source ledger**. If a valid unique fastest record also meets the independently approved absolute `T_FC` quality gate, it **claims current trophy possession then**; a pre-maturity Game can qualify retrospectively as the current fastest, but **never receives backdated possession for the immature period**. No qualifying contender => *unclaimed*.
3. **After maturity:** a **strictly lower** eligible projected Castle age-up clock (same approved comparison cohort and source model equivalence) transfers current possession **immediately upon current source acceptance** to the new record holder, provided the separate performance rule is satisfied. Equality is **not a challenge victory**. A later slower personal best or merely better Game of the challenger changes neither holder nor succession.
4. **On correction/withdrawal:** rebuild current record extrema from **current authoritative** Game revisions, then re-evaluate possession and its date-effective succession. If the challenger source was invalid, retract any false former-holder claim for the invalid interval, restore the prior rightful current holder **when still qualified**, and retain an independent correction audit entry. If the prior holder is also invalid, move to the next eligible record holder or unclaimed as warranted; never invent a winner. An accepted backdated Game is not permitted to create fabricated original transfer dates. Record played time, source acceptance/recognition time, and correction effective time separately.
5. **At Season end:** freeze a **factual archive** of the valid final holder, historical possession intervals and source/correction audit. Do not award a new end-of-Season-only competitive trophy or block legitimate pre-end transfers. Reopening a corrected Season archive requires a controlled revision preserving previous published audit visibility; do not alter a concluded Season merely from a duplicate upload.

**Simple states:** `RECORD_PROVISIONAL → TROPHY_PENDING_MATURITY → TROPHY_UNCLAIMED_OR_CURRENT_HOLDER → (STRICTLY_BETTER_GAME → TRANSFER) → SEASON_ARCHIVED`. `SOURCE_CORRECTED/WITHDRAWN → REBUILD_SOURCE_LEDGER → RESTORE_RIGHTFUL_HOLDER_OR_UNCLAIMED`. No state transition is authorization to write a production award.

**Ties: keep the genuinely open policy small.** The Record Book can show **all exact equal eligible co-recordholders**. A challenger who merely **ties** the current trophy holder does **not** displace them under the strict-better transfer rule; no arbitrary upload-order winner. For the **first maturity crossing**, if the best eligible result is a true tie **without an existing qualifying incumbent**, mark trophy possession **PENDING_TIE_POLICY** until the owner approves joint holding or another defensible solution. If a correction removes the incumbent and leaves multiple equal best eligible contenders, also withhold arbitrary assignment. The tie policy is still an owner choice; no fake first-arrival precedence or invented shared-emblem grant.

**Season maturity stability:** once an accepted mature baseline exists, later reduced counts caused by unrelated invalid Games must not silently award a weaker player; recompute maturity and eligibility from current source. If the baseline is no longer demonstrably mature, **withhold active possession pending review**, do not claim an older/current trophy holder was automatically qualified. When source authority is re-established, rebuild from accepted facts and an append-only correction audit. Result-pending is not a blanket veto for a genuinely source-qualified non-victory FC timing.

### Transfer proof and audit contract (design, NOT new production writes)

- **Identity and source:** stable Season ID; canonical Battle/Match ID; canonical Game ID (multiple Games distinct under same Battle); player ID; authoritative current replay source/hash and processing/revision; original source action witness; mapped map/start/civ/patch/model/format, actual comparison cohort; projected time and unit; accepting authority/time.
- **Selection:** per `AR2_REC_CASTLE` choose eligible **lowest Game** time; recompute all true tied extrema, compare to strict-better incumbent; keep mutable current record independent of trophy maturity. Incompatible model/version or unknown start is pending **only for that candidate**, not a global blackout.
- **Succession:** `seasonId,recordId,trophyInternalId,cohortId,holderPlayerId,sourceGameId,fromAcceptedAt,toAcceptedAt|null,status,recordValueMs,sourceRevision,revokedOrCorrectedReason`; historical audit separately tracks wrong old snapshots rather than treating invalidated holder intervals as valid honour. Do not collapse history into a current-holder Boolean.
- **No double awarding:** a Record Book holder can also legitimately have a personal achievement and private PB, but a trophy transfer is one current-possession transition, not another permanent earned badge or Battle/Event accomplishment. Rare **Distinctions** are permanent historical battlefield decorations only on independent executed-effect merit; never derived from FC, a temporary card, or social points.
