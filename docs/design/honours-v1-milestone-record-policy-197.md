**Important existing-classifier trap:** `AOF_BUILD_ORDER_V2` gives its Fast Castle candidate a score of 75 at **17:00**, a *hard maximum* at 18:00, but its classifier requires **executionScore >75** to qualify the named primary opening. Thus a 17:35 standard-start Castle projection can be within the classifier's stated hard maximum while still **not** be an accepted `Fast Castle` primary label. A 22:10 Castle projection is plainly not a plausible Fast Castle milestone. **Do not use `buildOrder.primaryLabel === Fast Castle` as the sole first-achievement gate**, nor automatically approve all Castle research requests. The new Initiate predicate must deliberately choose a source-qualified normal Fast Castle strategy/signature and an owner-calibrated **attainable** first-milestone clock; current V2 scoring and its threshold are *calibration/reference*, **not pre-approved achievement thresholds**. The final record of fastest projected Castle Age is a separate raw timing extreme, and the Elite Opulence branch has additional Boom conditions.

**Age source precision:** `opening.ageUp.castle.ageUpAtMs` = latest source-observed Castle research **request** + nominal 160,000ms. It is *not* observed Castle Age completion. A raw request alone, cancelled/contradicted research, missing source duration through the projected clock, unqualified mod/catalog/patch, unresolved starting conditions, duplicate POV, suspect Game binding or missing current READY revision cannot silently qualify. Suppress or mark `PENDING_EVIDENCE` for the affected record/award only. Result-pending does NOT itself disqualify source-valid opening behaviour, PB or non-victory timing records; official win/result-dependent honours remain held. Use an explicit source-confidence policy and honest `Projected Castle Age ≈` label unless a completed-age witness arrives.

## Comparability: ordinary map/civ variation is NORMAL, unusual starts are SPECIAL

Do **not** gate Fast Castle against an **identical map ID** or **identical civilization ID**. Ordinary maps/civs may enter one *standard-start competitive class* if the metric-specific starting opportunity, initial age, starting TC and resources, clock/game speed, patch/catalog model and supported source are genuinely comparable. Explicitly include **civilization effects and legitimate bonuses as normal AoE2 competitive expression** where they do not change the measurement or create a demonstrably different start; show civ/map in record evidence. Standard 1v1 and fixed teams can be evaluated under the same basic clock-start opportunity if genuinely equivalent, while preserving format labels and a drill-down. FFA may qualify when its opportunity is actually the same; this is not a blanket FFA exclusion or automatic inter-format equivalence.

**Separate or withhold direct timing comparisons** when there is a known materially different opportunity: **Nomad, Migration, custom/scenario starts, altered starting age/resources/TCs, unusually configured fish/nomadic/water economies, game-speed or data-mod differences, unqualified civ/model mechanics, or missing start proof**. An ordinary hybrid or water map is not automatically incomparable: test whether water economy materially alters this specific record, rather than assuming map ID determines the result. Dedicated **qualified cohort records** can still be displayed with their actual scope, but a special-start winner is never silently crowned the universal fastest standard-start champion.

Apply this same **opportunity-not-identity** reasoning to all 26 Record Book candidates by measurement family:
- **Time to action / age**: compatible starting opportunities and clock needed; ordinary map/civ IDs generally not a filter.
- **Checkpoint at 20/30m**: original source must span cutoff; civ/start/resource availability and metric model valid. Missing checkpoint ≠ zero.
- **Whole-Game maxima (farms, raids, buildings, economy zones, relic touches)**: raw record is historical **volume/exposure**, not demonstrated skill; disclose Game duration/opportunities. Do not silently compare a 2h FFA to a 15m 1v1 as equal proof of skill; use separate format/duration classes or a clearly qualified normalized-rate companion, not fabricated normalized raw values.
- **Rates/APM**: exact qualifying observed Game length, minimum credible duration, same action-class/model version; one short opening burst cannot become an all-game APM record. Show raw APM, not skill.
- **Social/team assistance**: helper→beneficiary→attacker and fixed team at incident; normalize against genuinely threatened-ally opportunities for any skill claim. No proof of an actual rescue.
- **Win streak / fastest victory**: final official outcome, correct Game vs Battle unit and season match chronology; BO3 Game wins must not become a 3-Battle streak; handle walkovers and unusual victory formats separately.
- **Mixed evidence model versions**: partition or explicitly version-compatible transform; never average incompatible models and call it one exact result.

**Stats Worker S #206 handoff**: The public Statistics code currently suppresses eligible leaders when a newer/incomplete player appears and `SeasonStatisticsHallV2.resolveRecord()` compares across context groups by model without feature-specific opportunity proof. Implement source-scoped holder cards with honest labels: `Qualified [standard-start] record`, `Qualified [special-start cohort] record`, or `No comparable evidence yet`, plus Games/Battles eligibility counts. Do not add a flood of badges. **A record is a single accepted Game extreme**; a true `Per Battle` mean/median should first collapse Games inside each canonical Battle according to metric family before a separate Season aggregation, but cannot replace the record's original Game source. A newcomer/unknown source must not erase valid qualified cohort records or manufacture absolute leadership.

## Swift Ascent trophy maturity — three owner-reviewable policy options

**Both absolute speed excellence and season/league maturity are REQUIRED**, independent of simply being current holder. All numerical inputs `T_FC` (absolute projected FC clock), `D_elapsed` (calendar or completed official Events), `N_distinctEligibleBattles`, `N_comparablePlayers` and optional `N_comparableGames` are deliberately `null` until owner approval.

| Option | When award is evaluated | Retrospective early Games | Prize count and meaning | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — End-of-Season laureate (RECOMMENDED)** | Only after official Season closes, mature independent competitor sample and fixed absolute `T_FC` are confirmed; take the current source-qualified **Season standard-start record holder(s)** at lock. | **Yes:** every current-valid compatible Game from that Season competes, irrespective of upload timing. Never first-uploader privilege. | At most one laureate *performance* per authorized Season/class; exact tied holders share. Later Seasons may produce new dated laureates, but don't retroactively unaward prior valid laureates. | Cleanest rare prestige; easy player understanding and correction; minor delay applies **only to trophy**, not Initiate/PB/public current record. |
| **B — Post-maturity record-breaker** | Once both elapsed-period and eligible-sample maturity gates are passed, a Game must **strictly surpass an already published compatible mature prior record** plus absolute standard. | **No** trophy for early pre-maturity records unless owner adds explicit later reconsideration. | Multiple later qualifying breaks can earn dated trophies; potential over-awarding, discourages early outstanding Games. | Possible but likely weaker fairness. |
| **C — Mature checkpoint champion + Season end** | One award per declared maturity checkpoint and/or final lock, independently tested against absolute standard. | **Yes** for contenders valid at each checkpoint. | Potential multiple laureates per Season if repeated checkpoints; requires explicit one-award frequency and transfer rules. | More complicated; no reason to implement before genuine Season sample. |

**Recommendation A**. Formula **only in design**: `TROPHY = SEASON_FINALIZED ∧ (elapsed_or_events ≥ D_elapsed) ∧ distinctEligibleBattles ≥ N_distinctEligibleBattles ∧ distinctComparablePlayers ≥ N_comparablePlayers ∧ champion_value ≤ T_FC ∧ no source/official dispute ∧ approved authoritative primary comparable cohort`. Coherent sample must include **independent** Battle/player identities; counting POV uploads or 3 Games from one Battle as multiple independent competitive opportunities is disallowed. Even if calendar age is long, a Season with one active player is **not mature**. No arbitrary trophy for first entry, a league record without absolute excellence, or unvalidated time.

**State flow:** `SOURCE_PENDING → CURRENT_QUALIFIED_RECORD (provisional current champion, no medal) → PENDING_MATURITY → MATURITY_CONFIRMED + ABSOLUTE_EXCELLENCE + SEASON_FINAL ⇒ DATED_TROPHY_LAUREATE`; source revision/dispute can move any state to `WITHHELD` and then re-evaluate. `NO_VALID_CHAMPION` or `NO_ONE_MEETS_EXCELLENCE` legitimately yields no Swift Ascent trophy for a Season. Never force annual winner. `THORN_EMPEROR` remains separately pinned official Emperor warmup, unconnected to FC timing.

**Source revisions, ties and correction:** exact equal values from two independently eligible Games in the *same comparable cohort* produce co-holders and, if both pass the eventual trophy policy, co-laureates (one tied performance, not arbitrary winner). A later superior Game in the same Season changes the **current** holder but never grants a trophy prematurely. A later valid Season can supersede the *all-time record* without revoking an earlier **legitimate season-vintage trophy**. A corrected winner's source or invalid measurement **removes their trophy claim**, and a historical award must be re-evaluated using the amended eligible Season ledger (other contenders may inherit if actually qualified). For owner-not-approved `retroactive` alternative, hold final choice rather than implement automatic backdating. Founding records can be public current records, but should not be misrepresented as **historical record-breaking trophies**.

**Multi-Game / POV counterexample:** Player D has FC 17:50 in Game 1 and 17:10 in Game 2 of a BO3 league Battle; only 17:10 may be D's single-Game PB and candidate record. A 19m Game cannot be used as zero for a 20m resource checkpoint, though a fully source-qualified FC at 16:00 in that 19m Game could still count.
