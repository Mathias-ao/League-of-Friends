## The smallest player-facing honours model

**Competition:** a trophy has `CURRENT_HOLDER | PENDING_MATURITY | UNCLAIMED | PENDING_SOURCE | ARCHIVED` plus a correction-aware historical holder succession. If a strictly better eligible result challenges the current measurable trophy record after its separate maturity/quality rule, **current possession transfers during the Season**; earlier possessors lose the visible current trophy but remain factual dated former holders if the original source stays valid. Tie with current holder does not dethrone them; first/no incumbent tie awaits owner rule. Do not conflate season-specific with league-wide scope.

**Identity:** one *equipped epithet* max, independently active/earned. A revoked/lost epithet is removed immediately from the display label, without deleting audited past eligibility. `the Unbroken` is *revocable current unbeaten status*, not a permanent badge; `Master of the Three Schools` depends on currently valid source-backed elite branch entitlements. Names are display text only and never replace player account identity.

**Merit:** a Distinction is awarded for one independently admitted historical deed. Repeated qualifying FFA sole victories **upgrade the same medal's emblem**; they do not mint a medal per Game. Other composite medals require completed source evidence or explicitly honest inferential labels, and may be permanently kept while original evidence stays valid. Neither a Battle/Game editorial card, an Event Roundoff card, an ordinary Reputation/Relationship track, nor private Chronicle sentiment automatically mints a medal.
