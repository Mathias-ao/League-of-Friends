## Cruelty: E-CRU-01 The Iron Harvest — Returning to the Fields

**V1 Chronicle meaning:** A repeatedly brings source-linked military pressure toward *B's economic locations* across distinct league Battles, returning to independent B-specific economic targets in the new Battle. This is an historical interpretation of *persistent punishing economic pressure*, not proven villager loss, economic deprivation, malice or weakened opposition.

**Boolean positive: all C1–C5.**

- **C1 Authority:** active accepted READY original/evidence revisions; mapped eligible human A/B; current stable server-authoritative league Battle ID; ordered source events, credible attacker A, target B, permitted role and inference method. A and B must actually oppose one another in the referenced episode, not merely occupy different nominal team rosters.
- **C2 Past:** at least one **earlier distinct league Battle**, not merely another Game/POV of the current Battle, contains an already source-linked and independently #126-admitted A→B **economic-targeted pressure** deed. Accurate pre-current-Battle history, direction, coverage and revision are required. Prior victory, rating, generic conflict or economy aggregate are not enough.
- **C3 Present:** current Battle contains **two** independently resolved, temporally ordered A→B economic-pressure episodes p and q. Raid V3/Pair Social Evidence retain exact original event IDs and A/B attribution; each separately passes the eventually owner-approved bounded *meaningful directed economic pressure* qualifier. The first closes before the second begins in authoritative Game/event order. Their source-deed lineages/parent command sets are disjoint and their B-specific economic target instances or spatial sites distinct. A continued raid, command spam, overlapping detector fragments or repeated retargeting is **one episode**.
- **C4 Role and substance:** economic target-role and B controller/ownership are qualified; weak nearest-opponent geometry, armed mixed-use buildings, Gold Control infrastructure influence, raw order counts, unsupported patch or unknown controller do not prove the role or source act. The inference is labelled **directed economic pressure**, never confirmed hits, deaths, destruction, lost resources or contemporaneous disadvantage.
- **C5 Historical/effect gate:** no material identity, chronology, episode-independence or source revision ambiguity; owner must expressly select this *new* Cruelty economic-persistence significance predicate and approve its calibration after genuine positive/negative corpus tests. Proposed/default-off state yields internal PREVIEW only; recognition is once per underlying pair×Battle turning point and does not bypass ordinary Event budgets.

**Explicit negatives:** (N-C1) same raid artificially split into two; (N-C2) repeated ATTACK clicks on one economic unit; (N-C3) target C rather than B or B's ownership unknown; (N-C4) ambiguous defensive/economic structure; (N-C5) two Games/duplicate POVs from one league Battle masquerading as historical recurrence; (N-C6) second episode not chronologically or source-lineage independent; (N-C7) earlier raid inferred solely from accepted outcome or lifetime aggregate; (N-C8) future weakness, ratings, a score decline or imagined actual losses read backward. Conclusive negative = NOT_QUALIFIED; missing required witness = UNAVAILABLE/PENDING; unapproved predicate = PREVIEW/UNCONFIGURED_POLICY.

**Plausible positive fixture, to be verified in authentic recordings:** Battle H records A→B source-qualified economic raid. Later Battle J contains A→B directed pressure against B's economy at site X; after its closure, an independent pressure episode at a different B economic target/site Y, with separately attributable source commands. Nothing here establishes B's economic weakness or losses. **Adversarial control:** a single ongoing raid with many retarget commands looks like two in a naive detector; one source-lineage collapse means NO exceptional event.

**Historical rarity:** Real earlier pair-economic targeting **plus** independently renewed economic pressure against the same opponent in another league Battle, not simply high APM or wins. If this occurs routinely in a real corpus, reject or recalibrate with explicit owner review rather than inflating a multiplier.

**Direction:** candidate is player-owned **A's Cruelty** only if a *separately approved ordinary* economic-persistence predicate qualifies; A→B Rivalry requires its own independent contest evidence. No B→A reciprocal hostility, no general hostility for raids and no automatic Bond reduction. This exceptional recognition itself causes **zero** numeric effects. No optional question or award.

**Scenes for #131, inferred scope preserved:**
- "Their earlier meeting had recorded A's pressure toward B's economy. In the next Battle, two separately qualified A→B economic-pressure episodes appeared at distinct B positions. Their material cost remains unknown."
- "For B, another encounter brought renewed A-directed pressure toward recorded economic targets. The annals already held an earlier such episode between the pair; they do not report unverified destruction."

**Strict retained variant:** E-CRU-01 / UPON_THE_WEAKENED still requires independent **pre-entry sustained disadvantage** before a genuinely separate A→B attack. The existing command pipeline does not provide sufficient comparable live force/resource state. Neither prior raid, future score, rating gap nor gold influence is a substitute. Keep held, not quietly reinterpreted as the V1 rule.

## Hostility: E-HOS-02 Old Wounds Reopened, alongside strict E-HOS-01 Broken Truce

**V1 Chronicle meaning:** A once supplied source-linked **recipient-specific help** to B. In another, later, genuinely opposed league Battle, A independently conducted both economic-targeted pressure and renewed separate directed antagonism against that same B. This is a rare *historical reversal of conduct*, **not a broken effective truce, proof of motive, real-world enmity or a special king-loss event**.

**Boolean positive: all H1–H6.**

- **H1 Authority:** qualified A/B identity, eligible current READY source, accepted canonical revisions, *distinct* league Battle IDs and reliable chronology; factual and inferred slots separately marked.
- **H2 Prior meaningful A→B help:** an **earlier distinct Battle** contains independently #126-qualified substantive or owner-approved bounded-inferred **A→B beneficiary-specific** aid, e.g. A's attributed DEFENSIVE_SUPPORT_WITH_PRESSURE for B under C→B pressure. Mere old same-team status, shared win, A/B shared target, previous aid **B→A**, or inferred Bond from a shadow engine fails. Existing independently verified A→B Bond may corroborate, not substitute.
- **H3 Genuine later opposition:** A/B officially opposed under known locked-team roster or separately qualified effective dynamic opposition; does **not** imply they were allied immediately beforehand, much less an effective rupture. Unresolved FFA effective stance does not silently become opposition.
- **H4 Serious current A→B directed conduct:** in the new Battle one independently meaningful, target-owned A→B economic-pressure incident p **and** one independent, later, B-specific military/economic-pressure incident q are established with original source IDs, distinct parent/deed lineages, meaningful actor A participation, non-overlapping chronology and no proximate pure self-defense classification. This is more than ordinary one-off warfare; generic results, target C or commands to defend against B cannot substitute for the two directed incidents.
- **H5 Distinct Hostility owner gate:** the owner must explicitly approve the combination of verified prior A→B help, later opposed alignment and renewed B-specific economic-directed antagonism as **distinct relational antagonism** beyond normal fighting. This new Hostility predicate **is not approved** merely because #122 accepts bounded normal social inference. Without approval, the contrast may be a non-awarding factual/inference-labelled historical candidate, not officially recognized Hostility.
- **H6 Integrity/priority:** no inferred historical cooperation from absence of contradictory facts; no episode double-split, wrong directional attribution, unsupported target/controller semantics, stale revised history or hidden Treachery collision. One main event per same turning point. Strict Treachery objective and victim-binding gates retain precedence unchanged.

**Explicit negatives:** (N-H1) A/B formerly same team but no qualifying A→B help; (N-H2) *B helped A*, not A helped B; (N-H3) later A directs pressure at C; (N-H4) one ordinary A→B raid or repeated clicks in a single incident; (N-H5) later A defends against an attack from B with no separate antagonistic action; (N-H6) alleged opposition/rupture only COMMAND_ONLY diplomacy; (N-H7) source evidence pending or wrong Battle sequence; (N-H8) B complains but A's conduct or the historical support is unsupported. Each must block official exceptional Hostility.

**Plausible positive fixture, genuine evidence still to be demonstrated:** Battle H records independent A→B defense participation in response to C's pressure on B's home, qualified as beneficiary-specific help. Later, officially opposed Battle J has source-attributed A→B economic pressure p and separate A→B directed pressure q. We infer the *change in the recorded conduct toward B* but neither effective alliance breach nor damage. **Adversarial control:** A/B won a previous team Battle without beneficiary-specific help; later A launches one ordinary economic raid on B. H2 and H4 fail; no Hostility.

**Rarity and effects:** unusual prior **A→B genuine help** plus separate later-Battle A→B independent repeated antagonism. A's current *action direction* is A→B; if owner approves H5 plus #126 conduct qualification, A→B **ordinary** Hostility may strengthen under #127 and independently A→B established Bond may weaken with a separate serious-antagonism rule. **B→A Hostility is not inherited** from being attacked or holding a historical Bond. Any stage-3+ reciprocity remains an independent same-track opposite-direction qualifying deed, not a victim-copy.

**Perspective question is optional, not an execution witness:** The existing #128 *situated grievance* family could ask B (only when this is the most meaningful eligible offer in B's durable respondent×Battle slot, respecting fatigue; routine 1v1 usually none): "Did A's recorded pressure leave a personal grievance, or did you see it as ordinary competition?" B's attributable choice may qualify an independently configured **B→A** judgment meaning under SOC-060. For Chronicle-derived reciprocity SOC-076–079 also require B's separate relevant directed conduct in **the same linked Battle**. B's words do not prove objective aggressive execution, effective truce rupture or A's motive. Pending judgments remain private, definitive Not Qualified is labelled accordingly. Never make that question a precondition for recording objective source history or a routine prompt.

**Scenes for #131, no invented betrayal:**
- "Earlier, the record placed A in a source-linked effort to help B defend their position. Now the two met as opponents, and A's attributed conduct returned to B in separate directed pressure episodes. Their motives remain unwritten."
- "What was once recorded as A's specific support of B now stood beside a later history of B-directed economic and military pressure. There was no proven truce to call broken."

**E-HOS-01 Broken Truce stays strict and held:** Requires qualified prior mutual effective Ally state, A-initiated effective rupture, independent ordered A→B distinct antagonism. COMMAND_ONLY changes or later opposing team status never qualify it. Existing Treachery additionally requires independently responsible king loss and victim's binding "betrayed" response; nothing here grants Treachery's sole extraordinary directional Hostility stage rule.
