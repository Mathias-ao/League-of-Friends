## A. What the Chronicle is and is not

The player-owned Chronicle is an annalist's selective account of independently accepted league history. Its primary narrative unit is **one owner × counterpart × canonical league Battle**, aggregating all accepted Games/POVs into one scene. It preserves the factual source and semantic uncertainty that Statistics and qualified source deeds actually provide. Historical callbacks reference **only genuinely earlier independent accepted Battles**; source corrections change scene content/revision without renaming the canonical scene ID. Older seen-history is retained by Sol's stable sceneId migration.

The same observed incident can enter Statistics, evidence-based social tracks, and the Chronicle **as a reference to one event**, but never three awarded achievements or independent dramatic episodes. Objective result, source observed command, bounded inferred conduct, independently qualified track receipt/stage, and an optional attributed private player's interpretation are **different classes of claim**. A grade candidate or Event spotlight is not a source fact and is never a chapter selector on its own.

**Source labels below:** O = accepted official Game/league-Battle result, roster, schedule, format, first/second exposure, match chronology; R = verified recorded action/command/statistics with model and original event lineage; I = source-method-admitted bounded conduct inference with independent context, not a proven physical outcome; H = prior-only authenticated independent Battle/source-event history; E = independently approved exceptional recognition ID + qualification receipt; J = authenticated, owner-private, specifically attributed player's perspective. **No I, E or J can be fabricated by the narrator.** For the current source method, method-admitted command conduct does not prove kills, damage, effective alliance, delivered tribute, completed buildings, superior skill or emotions. Pending/limited sources withhold only the affected statement.

## G. Six adversarial truth/correction acceptance cases

1. **One long raid, three correlated counts:** Raid V3 + skirmish + enemy-base contact share a source campaign; collapse them into one candidate episode. Must not tell three independent histories, mark triple extraordinary pressure or imply casualties.
2. **Tribute requested, receipt absent:** \`tributeSent\` and \`MATERIAL_AID_ORDER\` may show a requested amount; prevent “received”, “supplied” or “rescued”. All-zero control and missing effect witness hold only receipt claim.
3. **Castle foundation, not building:** \`castles\`, \`forward\` and BUILD command can establish placement; never write completed Castle, garrison, destruction or control; correct site ownership withdraws B-target wording.
4. **Official result delayed/revised:** Pair scene persists with result pending; on accepted official correction recompute the winner-related clause and downstream historical callback. Do not re-create sceneId; revise sceneRevision and seen-history without pretending a new Battle.
5. **Two POVs, BO3 and late upload:** Deduplicate exact source/GUID and group Games to one canonical Battle; later upload does not rewrite earlier scene with facts learned *after* the Battle. Source revision replacing prior evidence is not an additive feat.
6. **Two contradictory personal interpretations:** A and B can attribute different meanings to the same qualified pressure; objective conduct sentences unchanged. Neither speaker's private grievance, silence or decline proves the other's intent, receipt, damage, reciprocity or effective alliance. No public accusation.
