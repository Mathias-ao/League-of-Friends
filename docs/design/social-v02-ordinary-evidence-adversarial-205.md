## Executive judgment — gameplay must make sense before a green test counts

The owner's v0.2 *semantics* have changed, but the available *original replay facts* have not. Correct ordinary product design is **not** “every raid = Cruelty” or “every 1v1 attack = Hostility”; nor is it “only a disadvantaged offensive charge = Gallantry.” To reflect real AoE2 play, a contextual grade should distinguish **competitive skill**, **oppressive tactic choice**, **protective intervention**, **neutral-zone mutual contest**, **invasion of a named opponent's economic/home area**, and **real inter-player historical assistance or breach**.

**The central product test:** two players can have legitimate, source-bounded ordinary competitive Rivalry on Arabia and a different-civ/map rematch on Arena without being required to repeat the *identical* map, civilization or arbitrary Game-duration bucket. Exact starting state/patch/map/geometry still matters **locally** to prove a specific opportunity (e.g., forward placement relative to a genuinely known TC, recorded neutral zone, opening timing or boar laming). It must not globally prevent longitudinal character/relationship accumulation across regular play. Truly different opportunities (Nomad/Migration/custom starts, naval, FFA dynamic diplomacy) qualify **only the affected deeds/axes**, not erase unrelated pair contest or protected evidence. A missing winner does not nullify independently source-admitted conduct unless the particular deed requires official outcome.

## Source modality and grades (design, not activation)

- `OBSERVED`: exact original `sourceEventId`, actor, original clock/ordinal, recorded `ORDER`/`BUILD`/`TRIBUTE`, original target if qualified. It means **command observed**, not damage, completed construction, actual villager killed, resource delivered, Wonder built or relic possessed.
- `BOUNDED_INFERENCE`: Raid V3 local economy-zone pressure; Engagement V4 Skirmish/Battle/assist; Pair Episode Context V1; Context V2 per-opponent placement/TC geometry (territory policy currently **UNAPPROVED**, home anchor enumeration **nonexhaustive**). Only the named #173 method passing its *independent* linked predicates may produce `SOURCE_ADMITTED` private `ORDINARY`; otherwise `PENDING`.
- `RESULT_VERIFIED`: official bound Game winner or server pre-match Elo snapshot is independent **match authority**, not replay damage, manners, played counter-unit results, or an automatic social source grade.
- `SUBJECTIVE`: an invited participant's author-private stated experience of a **source-grounded** incident. **Not** a source witness or proof of the other player's intent, a successful attack or an act by the reverse direction.
- `PENDING`: required source, complete alternative opportunities, effective FFA relation, prior B weakness, completed effects, owner policy or chronology missing. Keep other qualified tracks visible; never silently replace with numeric 0.
- `+`/ `++`: proposed meaning of qualifying ordinary/substantial strengthening; `−`/ `−−`: separately qualified positive weakening act. **No auto-++**, no automatic null-vs-zero equivalence, no global sign balancing. Pilot `ORDINARY` alone remains `PROPOSED_EMULATOR_ONLY` and not a public entitlement.

### Neutral vs invasive is not a simple square on the map

- **Positive neutral contest** requires actual source-backed targeted command contribution by **A against B** near independently qualified contested land and enough map/starting-anchor evidence to distinguish B's home from genuinely shared ground. `Engagement V4` has `skirmishes[].center` and source participant edges, but **Context V2 `_territory` currently classifies per-opponent *BUILD placements*** using explicit **unapproved** distance policy; it does not already provide a source-admitted neutral-zone **combat** metric. A newly named and genuinely tested method must check genuine map/patch/build/clock source; do not reuse a placement “neutral” classification as a battle.
- **Positive B-home/eco pressure** can use Raid V3 originally attributed B economic zones or direct target-controller evidence, **with explicit `BOUNDED_INFERENCE`**: this establishes commands consistent with A targeting B's economy, not killed Villagers, a breached wall, actual working tower, proven suffering or B feeling angry. Attributing an *invasive context* is plausible; promoting **Hostility `+`** requires a separately approved method addressing intensity/selectivity, counterevidence, chronology and alternatives. A classic aggressive 1v1 strategy is not automatically emotional hostility.
- **Neutral and invasive evidence can coexist** within one Battle or even original source-connected episode. Track grades remain independent. **Defensive counterplay is actor-owned** (B must actually have B original command/target evidence); the fact of being raided creates B *opportunity*, not a Rivalry credit. An attacker using advanced strategy can be skilful **Gallantry** and tactically oppressive **Cruelty** without any assertion of motives, even if neither novel method is yet source admitted.
- **Opportunity normalization is per specific deed**, not one universal ratio: opponent-choice denominator = actually independently observable *reachable alternatives* during the choice window (not all roster enemies); aid opportunity = beneficiary-specific earlier threat + A access; opening skill = suitable start/build/civ/patch model; gold position = actual same original cluster and source-referenced placement; repeated pressure = distinct actor-owned incidents/Battles, not one long raid split into many. Unknown target reach/fog-of-war information leaves selectivity **PENDING** rather than an assumed 100% focus rate.

## Game/Battle/Event and cross-context accumulation rules

1. **Episode qualification vs aggregate trend:** game-local original action provenance binds a deed. Year-long progression aggregates *independent admitted source episodes*, not `raidsOut`/APM/queue sums, and never needs the **same exact ordinary map ID or civilization** to describe a player or directed pair. Opportunity comparability is family-specific and local: standard-start FC vs Nomad is different; a valid opponent-attributed raid on another ordinary map remains a valid pair contest. Other unsupported fields do not poison the rest of a Game.
2. **Opportunities and ‘not shown’:** qualified count/opportunity rate can be contextual quality, but don't universally divide ordinary source grades by Game minutes, enemy count, total Battlegrounds or extra Games in a series. Raw contributions across more Games can legitimately matter historically, while **skill/selectivity** requires independently observed comparable opportunities. Missing is `PENDING`, *not* zero or negative. Only a genuinely sampled *eligible* denominator permits a comparative interpretation; never treat the whole lobby as the set of reachable opponents.
3. **Distinct identity layers:** original `sourceEventId` + `sourceDeedId` / `incidentId` → source-connected contribution in **Game GUID/source revision**. One Game may have multiple independently sourced episodes; two POVs of the same Game have the **same** source; two Games in one Battle are distinct Game results but the same **Battle** for #139 Reputation budget and one Battle historical exposure. Event relationship budgets and independently eligible Event actor witness requirements remain owned by #139.
4. **Authoritative chronology:** use [#176](https://github.com/Mathias-ao/League-of-Friends/pull/176) approved Event scheduling, MatchPlan Battle slot and Game number; no upload time, arbitrary file timestamp or id sort for cross-Battle first/previous/after. The **original within-Game ordinal** is useful for pre-entry threat vs later act *inside* the Game but does not establish cross-Game Event order.
5. **Independent reciprocity:** [#139](https://github.com/Mathias-ao/League-of-Friends/blob/main/functions/src/engines/socialV1History/history.js) `canUp()` checks source-backed eligible Event credits **from BOTH A→B and B→A** at stage 3+, and existing Rivalry stage-3 floor on weakening remains. Merely being raided (victim), mutual sentiment, dual POV, a narrator paragraph or the mere opportunity to attack is not B's authentic actor-owned deed. Stage 1–2 can be one-sided.
6. **Source correction is selective:** source withdrawal, corrected target owner, revised detector/model, mapped player, actual current GUID, League rights, official result, source custody or scheduling change re-runs the appropriate method and canonical #139 history. A removed A→B Raid method retracts affected R and its scene **but should not wipe an unrelated valid Bond/Chivalry assistance in the same Battle**, or all another player's source-backed metrics merely because the winner remains Pending.
7. **AI and custom modes:** [Statistics experience](../design/statistics-experience.md) preserves AI warm-up Battle Statistics but not human-opponent Relationship grades. Fixed teams require complete authentic lobby roster/teams. FFA dynamic effective relations remain Pending without a method; special custom starts affect beginning-TC/age/neutral-zone opportunity rather than erasing every source exact original fact. Water engagement/target model scope (Fishing Ship/Trade Cog) needs distinct opportunity from land base pressure; **no universal land-TC normalization**.
