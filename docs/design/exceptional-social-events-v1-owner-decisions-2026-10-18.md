## Six exact event cards

### 1. Gallantry — Against the Tide · `E-GAL-01`

**Historical meaning.** A twice-defeated challenger faces B again and takes an active offensive role. The league records the challenge, not a mythical battlefield power imbalance or a guaranteed heroic victory.

**Positive, all:** G1 = prior **two distinct officially decided 1v1 league Battles** between A and B, both accepted B wins over A and known *before* the present Battle; G2 = A and B genuinely oppose each other in current eligible Battle; G3 = one independently admitted, material, source-linked A→B offensive episode with A's own directed evidence and a versioned **past-only** commitment or attack-substance measure; G4 = all Q0 and independently reviewed Gallantry significance rule. Current result may be defeat; no win requirement. **Two preceding setbacks is a proposed historical rarity gate, not an approved scoring threshold.**

**Negative:** G−1 = only generic rating/Season standings, team loss, same-Battle Games or retrospective losing score; G−2 = offensive ORDER/click with no admitted significant own conduct; G−3 = another teammate's attack or target not B; G−4 = claimed material inferiority from pre-Match rating or later game result. **Authentic feasibility:** official results and Raid/Skirmish/Engagement candidate lines exist; #126 needs real actor/ordinal/episode and source-measurement positive and adjacent no-substance control. If no genuine witness, **PENDING**, not "brave" by Elo.

**Rarity:** repeated personal setbacks **then** sourced offensive initiative. **Chronicle:** “Twice B had prevailed over A in the league record. At their next meeting A again directed a substantial challenge toward B. The annals cannot measure courage, but they can remember the undertaking.”

**Correction:** changing either earlier outcome, source or actor-owned offense retracts/requalifies this event on canonical rebuild; changing later result alone does not erase a still-proved attempt unless it invalidates admission.

### 2. Cruelty — The Iron Harvest: Returning to the Fields · `E-CRU-01/RETURNING_TO_FIELDS`

**Historical meaning.** A campaigner returns to the *same opponent's economy* across separate Battles and renews the pressure within the new Battle. “Iron Harvest” names persistent conduct, **not** actual devastation, helplessness, confirmed villager deaths, or a negative judgment of A's real character.

**Positive, all:** C1 = a prior *different league Battle* contains source-linked, independently admitted A→B economic-targeted pressure; C2 = current opposed Battle contains **two distinct** A→B economic pressure incidents p and q; C3 = each has own A source events, qualified B target/controller and permitted economic role with meaningful #126 bounded offense admission; C4 = p ends before q starts in authoritative source order, disjoint source-deed/parent lineages and distinct B economic targets/sites, not one prolonged raid; C5 = owner-selected extraordinary economic-persistence category and Q0. **No victim-weakness condition is asserted.**

**Negative:** C−1 = raid click spam, one detector episode split, q overlaps p, or a duplicate POV; C−2 = C's economy, generic enemy geography, unknown B controller or mixed armed TC/Castle relabelled vulnerable economy; C−3 = “prior” Game in the same Match; C−4 = inferred B weakness from future score, Elo, Gold Control influence, defeat or A's earlier pressure. **Authentic feasibility:** Raid V3 episodes/economic-target types and neutral source lineage exist; real controlled opponent/target separation and episode-independence cases have **not** been proven accepted by #126. Ask Worker 4 for a pinned authentic pair-raid positive and same-campaign negative. If absent, **pending launch qualification**.

**Rarity:** A targeted B's economy in an earlier Battle and genuinely renewed separate targeted economic actions in a later Battle, rather than being a standard raider. **Chronicle:** “An earlier field had already seen A's pressure directed toward B's economic positions. Another Battle records A returning twice to distinct positions of B's economy. The losses, if any, remain beyond the record.”

**Correction:** if any necessary earlier deed, target controller, current episode independence or Battle ID changes, rescind/pend the label and rewrite dependent Chronicle prose; retain neutral source facts still valid.

**Strict future subtype remains held:** `E-CRU-01/UPON_THE_WEAKENED` requires independently demonstrated **pre-entry** sustained economic/military disadvantage before a new attack. Existing evidence does not prove this. Never silently promote the V1 recurrence proxy to actual weakness.

### 3. Chivalry — The Shield at the Gate · `E-CHI-01`

**Historical meaning.** While C pressures B's home area, A takes an independent and attributable part in B's defense. This is a remembered intervention, **not proof B was rescued, actual damage prevented or units destroyed**.

**Positive, all:** CH1 = source-linked C→B threat and local contest at reliably anchored **B home**; CH2 = A's own source-event IDs and independent directed defensive intervention refer to that same qualified threat/beneficiary B; CH3 = interval/order supports contribution while B is threatened, with per-actor source semantics and #126 **meaningful directed help** admission; CH4 = A not merely nearby/on roster/co-attacking C elsewhere, plus Q0. The context must be noteworthy beyond routine team alignment under an approved Chivalry rule.

**Negative:** CH−1 = mere A/B team membership, orders near B's base without qualified link, A defending only A's base; CH−2 = C pressure inferred from unverified nearest geometry or incident without own actor source; CH−3 = “save” inferred from B's survival; CH−4 = double counted shared attack/defense. **Authentic feasibility:** Engagement V4 defense participation and `DEFENSIVE_SUPPORT_WITH_PRESSURE` source-link context exist; #126 has not shown genuine supported *meaningful help* from an accepted original. Controlled team replay with timed C→B pressure, A's own defense action and nearby unrelated-attack negative is smallest positive/negative witness.

**Rarity:** identifiable beneficiary + home threat + distinct participating defender rather than routine teammate fight. **Chronicle:** “As C's recorded pressure reached B's home ground, A joined a source-linked defensive encounter there. The records do not say whether B was saved, only that A took part beside the threatened player.”

**Correction:** altered threat actor, home anchor, A's participant ownership or withdrawn READY source suspends recognition; optional B perception never repairs missing conduct proof.

### 4. Rivalry — The Reckoning · `E-RIV-01`

**Historical meaning.** An established head-to-head history changes course in a fresh encounter where *both* players actually contend.

**Positive, all:** R1 = B had previously beaten A in **two distinct accepted 1v1 league Battles** (a proposed recognition rarity gate); R2 = in a new accepted 1v1 Battle A now beats B, with version-consistent official result; R3 = independent source-admitted A→B contest and **B→A** active contest or substantive defensive resistance (B must own its evidence); R4 = stable chronological pair-history evidence and Q0. A two-sided encounter is not inferred from a two-player roster or result alone.

**Negative:** R−1 = multi-Game Match results counted as multiple previous Battles; R−2 = an opposing team win treated as personal defeat of B; R−3 = passive B, victims copied as respondent aggression; R−4 = a corrected previous result reverses the supposed turn. **Authentic feasibility:** official 1v1 results are comparatively strong; real source-owned two-direction contest still needs #126 accepted action/defensive materiality against passive-target negative. If R3 remains missing, show ordinary outcome reversal without exceptional Rivalry label.

**Rarity:** a qualified two-sided **reversal** after recurring earlier personal defeats. **Chronicle:** “B had twice prevailed in their earlier league meetings. This time A prevailed, and both left an independent record of the contest. The old record had found its reckoning.”

**Correction:** a changed current/prior result or an invalidated B-owned defensive action retracts the exceptional label while leaving any separately valid rematch facts; never fabricates mutual stage or bypasses Rivalry's protected floor.

### 5. Hostility — Old Wounds Reopened · `E-HOS-02`

**Historical meaning.** A had once given B directed assistance. Later, *as genuine opponents*, A repeatedly brought directed pressure against that same B. The stark reversal from help to antagonism is the historical milestone—not proof of grudge, unfairness, betrayal or a currently broken truce.

**Positive, all:** H1 = independent, admitted **A→B** beneficiary-specific help in a *different prior Battle* (not B→A help, mere former teammate status or nominal Bond); H2 = current officially opposed A/B sides, or genuinely qualified dynamic opposition, **not command-only diplomacy**; H3 = current A→B, source-linked, economically targeted p plus **distinct later** A→B military/economic pressure q with own A events, independent disjoint episode lineages, known B target and #126 meaningful antagonism coverage; H4 = explicit **owner approval** that H1+H2+H3 constitutes relationally **distinct Hostility beyond ordinary warfare**; H5 = Q0, precedence and correction fences. The prior assistance-to-targeted-antagonism arc must actually be demonstrated, not reconstructed from role labels.

**Negative:** H−1 = old shared team/victory or B helped A, with no meaningful A→B help; H−2 = current single routine raid, continued p relabelled q, generic warfare or victim impact copied as B→A conduct; H−3 = C was target, ambiguous opponent, purely A's self-defense, or old “ally” inferred solely from COMMAND_ONLY; H−4 = later B claims grievance but independent A conduct is absent. **Authentic feasibility:** military pair episodes exist, but *prior directed meaningful aid* has no verified original-to-qualifier positive; neither has independently qualified hostility significance. **Highest evidence/approval risk.** Controlled **two-Battle** 3-player team-then-opposed recording sequence with A→B real support under C pressure, then separate p/q with B ownership and one routine-opposition negative is the smallest meaningful witness. Until then **pending**.

**Rarity:** a source-linked **change in one player's direction toward a former beneficiary**, with renewal beyond routine warfare, not one negative act between scheduled opponents. **Chronicle:** “In an earlier field A had been recorded lending B specific aid. In a later war they faced one another, and A twice directed separate pressure episodes toward B. The ledger marks the change; it cannot tell why.”

**Direction and correction:** present action is A→B; it cannot automatically assign B→A Hostility, reciprocity or mutual Feud. Separate B grievance testimony under #128 is optional, author-attributed and never proves attack or truce. If old A→B help, current p/q or opposed alignment is revised/withdrawn, event becomes pending/retracted and subsequent history recomposes.

**Strict `E-HOS-01` Broken Truce remains dormant:** demands true **mutual effective prior alliance**, A-initiated **effective rupture**, independent post-rupture directed antagonism, qualified chronology/mode. A declaration alone cannot qualify. **Treachery stays separate and unchanged:** independently attributed former ally's king loss, strict rupture/association and victim's binding “betrayed” judgment are mandatory for its sole nonreciprocal stage exception; no shortcut, copy, duplicate header or stage jump through Old Wounds Reopened.

### 6. Bond — Stand Together · `E-BON-01`

**Historical meaning.** Years or Battles apart, one player's aid is returned by the other. The reciprocal deed is what makes the shared history remarkable.

**Positive, all:** B1 = a previous distinct league Battle contains independently qualified meaningful **A→B beneficiary-specific** assistance; B2 = a later Battle contains independently qualified **B→A** meaningful directed assistance with B's own actor events and A the attributable beneficiary; B3 = each qualifying directed help is independently sourced and not merely two actors co-attacking C, standing near each other or copying the recipient's own commands; B4 = reliable historical order, current-source coverage and Q0. Formal alliance not mandatory if recipient-specific help is independently qualified; unresolved FFA relation/benefit stays pending.

**Negative:** B−1 = only A helped B twice (no reverse action), co-victory/teammate assignment, synchronized offense against C, unconfirmed tribute request presented as actual resource receipt; B−2 = an economic help target whose recipient cannot be mapped; B−3 = source reupload/multiple Games within one Battle mistaken for independent cross-Battle help. **Authentic feasibility:** defensive assistance context is available, but independently qualified **two-direction beneficiary-specific meaningful help** has not been shown from two authentic Battles. Controlled team aid in one Battle and reversed team aid in another, plus co-target/proximity and tribute-request-only negatives, is minimum evidence acceptance.

**Rarity:** independent cross-Battle return of help, not ordinary cooperative membership. **Chronicle:** “Once A had come to B's support. In a later field B returned meaningful help to A, each act grounded in a separate source. Their deeds now answer one another; the Chronicle offers no claim about friendship beyond them.”

**Correction:** withdrawal of either direction's source proof removes the reciprocal exceptional header, though a surviving one-way assistance chapter remains factual; no reciprocal stage created merely from this recognition.
