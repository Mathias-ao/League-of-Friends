## I. Make the collection appealing without badge inflation

**The experience should feel like learning a craft, not grinding an account level.** A new player can earn an opening, first relic interaction, walls or TC expansion in a real match. Returning players can beat their own best (privately in Chronicle) while accumulating rank-independent tiers. Specialists pursue **qualitatively harder** mastery branches. Elite School achievements open the optional Three Schools epithet. Trophies and military Distinctions remain separate, scarce and source-proven.

**Four proposed rank names** (not owner-approved): Initiate → Practiced → Expert → Elite. Fixed achieved-rank thresholds must eventually be *numbers or precise predicates chosen and versioned by the owner*, not dynamically scaled to leaderboard ranks. **Calibration METHOD**: stratify genuinely accepted original source Games by start/civ/map/mode/patch/clock and opportunity; inspect candidate empirical p50/p80/p97 levels to design stable tier cutoffs; require difficult *structural* predicates for Elite, not merely being in a percentile. Quantiles are tuning guidance, **never the live gate**. Rank names, numbers and variants not automatically authoritative from this document. For cumulative distinct-Battle families an illustrative 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 qualifying Battle pacing ladder is **NOT APPROVED**, may be excessively long for Season I; prefer lifetime accumulation and owner-scaled Season length.

**Collection design:** three browsable shelves: *The Openings* (Drush, Scouts, Archers, FC, Water), *Fieldcraft* (Relics, Gold Influence, Scouting, Raiding, Walls), *The Realm and Fellowship* (TCs, Farms, Military Commitment, Fish Traps, Defensive Assists). Each family has one tiered primary badge, muted earned rank pips and at most one advanced-branch ribbon. A family card reveals `What counts`, `What does not`, mode/map applicability, best Game/source Battle, next rank target **only when approved**, data-certainty state and reason for any unavailable coverage. Personal bests must stay **private, owner-only** in the Chronicle self leaf; do not leak private PB values through public achievement progress. Optional high-level achievements can have explicitly approved public display separate from PB detail. Do not display "zero progress" when source coverage is missing; show `Not yet measurable`. Once legitimately earned, a tier is retained across weaker performances, but corrected false sources withdraw it.

**Prestige economy:** one progression family per concept, not another badge for every stat checkpoint or every record-holder. A Battle permits at most four editorial accomplishment cards; an Event Roundoff shows four–five selected from its existing **64 ranked** catalogue with family collapse and no filler. Neither spotlight is a permanent trophy, medal, School entitlement, Reputation score or Relationship point. The same qualified incident can be cited in Chronicle and counted once in its legitimate tier family; its appearance on several screens is *not* extra credit. An explicit later-approved trophy condition can independently reward that incident, but do not infer automatic eligibility because an Event had a top-ranked card.

### Precise rank calculations and anti-farming

Let `G(p)` be **currently accepted** Games with current authenticated READY source, eligible original player mapping, complete metric-specific coverage, matching comparator cohort, non-disputed result and supported clock. For BEST_GAME metric `m` with `dir` in {MIN, MAX}, `PB(p,m)=extreme{ value(g,m) : g∈G(p) }`; the rank is `max{tier : tier.fixedPredicate(current eligible game evidence) == QUALIFIED}`. A score on the BEST Game must pass that tier's structural gate; cannot mix the *time from Game 1* with *two TCs from Game 2* to fabricate an FC Boom.

Let `B(p)` be **distinct accepted league Match/Battle IDs** after Game+POV collapse. For RAIDS, `eligibleRaidBattle(p,b)` requires at least one separately source-qualified source-actor→victim episode in `b`. Its career milestone count is `sum over distinct b of 1{eligibleRaidBattle(p,b)}`. For DEFENSIVE_ASSISTS, use a qualified helper→beneficiary→threat **incident key**, followed by `one-or-more admitted assists per Battle`; attribution/opportunity can support advanced branch gates. **Never count 30 replays of one Battle as 30 career feats.**

**Calibration proposals, explicitly NOT thresholds:** first accepted and meaningful Game for Initiate; p50 / p80 / p97 of *comparable admitted performances* as a starting place for discussing stronger ranks; add time/site/opportunity minimums as independent safeguards. For speed/idle **lower is better**; 20m checkpoints require measured through 20m; volume metrics need a minimum denominator and feasible exposure, otherwise holding a Game open indefinitely can artificially improve the metric. For cumulative family demo only, 1/2/4/8 distinct Battles is a **rejected-as-policy-until-corpus-reviewed sketch**, not an implementation value. Neither p97 nor 1/2/4/8 should ever appear as a production expression without owner approval. In V1, a still-null tier must yield `PENDING_EVIDENCE / NOT_CONFIGURED`, never silently "earned".

**Difficulty balance:** water-only and team-only families are not mandatory for general progression or Master of the Three Schools. If a water-map or endangered ally opportunity is absent, the family is not "failed". Use stable cohorts and opportunity denominators for maximum fairness but never confuse `missing` with `0`. A rare high-rank achievement should not depend on holding a public league record; several players can achieve the same elite rank.

## V. Social history and Chronicle without accidental award multiplication

Shared causal join key proposal: `{canonicalBattleId, canonicalGameId, actorPlayerId, counterpartPlayerId|null, sourceEpisodeFamily, stableEpisodeId, currentSourceRevision}`. Keep **actor→beneficiary/opponent direction** for social meaning; one incident may have a raid response, a wall command and a garrison ORDER, but linked facts do not produce three independent rescues, three Bond grants or three medals. The six Reputation/Relationship dimensions are independent, and relationship direction B→A cannot be invented from A→B. Genuine Chivalry/Bond predicates need specific threatened ally, helper action, significance and independent source, not a larger defensiveAssist season count. Ordinary opponents fighting does not create Hostility, and two allies attacking the same target does not create Bond.

Chronicle can describe *the command evidence and the meaningful qualified historical context* where appropriate, and a rare human interpretation can add opt-in literary judgement; it must not invent actual kills, completed Castles, rescue success, tribute receipts, aggression motive or reconciliations. Corrections re-evaluate factual scene identities and associated award rights. A private PB can yield a self-leaf passage without publishing private Game values. Personality descriptions remain **descriptive**, not medal reasons.

## VI. Five worked cases (fictional, not observed league results)

1. **Fast Castle three surfaces:** A has a compatible **projected** Castle arrival 14:48 (current Season record), B 15:21 (B's private Chronicle PB). B separately qualifies *some* fixed Fast Castle tier when future owner cutoffs are approved; B does not need to defeat A to earn it. When B later posts 14:39 in a fully qualified Game, the new Game can change public holder, improve B's PB and upgrade a tier separately. B gets **no trophy** unless it breaks a *previously published compatible all-time* Castle record and `The Swift Ascent` is approved. Never merge "Fastest Castle RESEARCH START" with inferred projected arrival.
2. **Team helper, two social tracks, no automatic medal:** In a fixed-team 2v2 A gives source-qualified defensive assistance to B during B's opponent-attributed raid. The same credited assistance Battle can progress A's `AR2_ACH_DEFENSIVE_ASSISTS` **once**; the Season engagement metric may also report the episode. Bond A→B and/or A's Chivalry can be independently *considered* only if the richer social evidence qualifies, not because A's achievement or an Event spotlight exists. With command-only data, do not claim an actual saved Villager or award Shield of the Fellowship.
3. **Displayed prestige:** After separately earning eligible title and independently qualifying a dated public trophy, C chooses `C, Master of the Three Schools` + a compact trophy emblem whose tooltip opens the source Battle. Even if C has many earned badges, only the elected epithet and the few explicitly curated prestige emblems appear beside the name; the rest live in collection. If a School witness is later invalidated, equipped title withdraws while the unaffected trophy remains valid.
4. **Two Games, one Battle, duplicate POV:** A 2v2 league Match/Battle has Game 1 and Game 2. A qualified 18:20 FC in Game 1 and 17:55 FC in Game 2 means ONE FC PB candidate per Game, with personal best = 17:55, not an arithmetic 36:15 aggregate. Duplicate player POVs for Game 2 are the **same canonical Game**; if A raids in both Games, the `CUMULATIVE_DISTINCT_BATTLES` raid milestone gains **one** Battle contribution, not two or four.
5. **Record correction and near-miss:** A's purported 14:39 official record is invalidated because the Castle research was cancelled. The current record resolver recomputes actual qualified Game extrema, rolls back A's private PB if needed, retracts its false public holder and dependent trophy/tier, and restores the next valid holder. Legitimate old holder B becomes current again; A must **not** be celebrated as a "former valid record holder" for the invalid Game. Similarly a Villager-targeting `ORDER` near a threatened Ally is not automatically a material rescue, and a quickwall `WALL` order without a finished barrier is not a proven blocked raid.
