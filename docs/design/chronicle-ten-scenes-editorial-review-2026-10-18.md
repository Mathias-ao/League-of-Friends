## Editorial contract

These pages follow one recurring historical pair, **Aldric ↔ Beren**, plus the first meeting of **Mara ↔ Cato**, over ten distinct hypothetical league Battles. The exact spellings are invented for fixtures and are not claims about real league members. Cato also appears as an opponent in team and FFA contexts. "Battle" denotes the canonical league Battle, not one Game, replay file, command, or player POV.

**Common authority required before any scene:** active, integrity-verified, authorized READY source(s); accepted current Game/source revision and league roster mapping; one canonical Battle ID across Games; correct event, format, map, team/diplomacy mode and participants; dated/qualified historical order for callbacks; no withdrawn source, duplicate POV or invalid identity; and source-linked semantic beats. Public Chronicle prose may not expose source IDs, source hashes, private testimony, internal shadow stages or diagnostics. An UNRESOLVED result never becomes a winning or losing claim.

Evidence kinds used below:
- **O — objective/official:** authenticated roster/map/team status, admitted diplomacy **declarations** as declarations, accepted authoritative Battle result, independently demonstrated effective alliance if available.
- **S — qualified source conduct:** the existing semantic episode type, with attributable actor/target, source-event lineage and sufficient coverage. A reinforcement order is still only an order; participation is still not a rescue; an offensive attempt is not proven economic damage.
- **I — bounded inference:** a separately approved, versioned military/territorial/assistance inference with actor, counterpart, temporal and spatial constraints and confidence/coverage. It cannot be written as direct execution or measured loss.
- **H — historical callback:** independent, accepted earlier league-Battle entries and any explicitly eligible past standing/result. Direction is retained. No later Battle or current standing is back-written into a former page.
- **T — player testimony:** a structured, authenticated, binding response with speaker and visibility status; always a labelled quotation or inset, never converted to an objective narrator's assertion.

The scenes below deliberately distinguish **book-ready from semantic inputs** and **future-dependent editorial copy**. Existing C1 #140 only varies previously admitted V2 episode/callback prose (on at least two earlier distinct qualified Battles in the appropriate family and direction) and does not compose wins, losses, map/place names, inferred gold control, new exceptional classifications, testimony, or finished Castle claims. Worker 5's #136 currently provides an OFFICIAL/UNRESOLVED status but **not a winner field for the composer**, and its proposed scene/legacy-ID/multi-Game result contract is still unaccepted. Do not sneak result prose into #140 from game scores or raw replay statistics.

## Editorial checks across the ten pages

1. **A real history accumulates:** B01–B04 build a contest from accepted official results; B06–B07 introduce directed defence in opposing directions; B08 records one common opponent without inventing cooperation; B09 preserves an unproven attempt and pending result; B10, in a separate Regicide Battle, is exceptional **only** if the much stronger source and victim testimony gate genuinely passes.
2. **Truth beats ornament:** ordinary page B05 receives one sentence; it does not have an artificial second paragraph, an award or a Bond. Result and skill are not interchangeable. An attractive sentence cannot repair incomplete chronology, uncertain target ownership, unresolved outcome or missing source.
3. **Historical perspective is temporally local:** a reader may later know something that the narrator could not legitimately claim on B01. Future B10 severity does **not** change the wording or moral judgement of B01–B09. Backdated corrections legitimately recompose the corrected scene and impacted later context, preserving stable Battle/scene identity where Worker 5's final compatibility rule permits.
4. **Poetry stays sparse:** "the ledger tilts," "one banner," "the direction returned," and "beside the crown" carry specific evidentiary contrasts; only a fully qualified crown-loss episode receives the stronger finishing cadence. Humour, if used, belongs in a rare restrained aside, never mockery of an unsupported defeat.
5. **Human perspective remains a signature, not the narrator:** an authenticated player may remember perceived rescue, betrayal, skill or reconciliation; that meaning can appear only with correct author attribution/status, never as objective proof of a casualty, received aid, opponent's intent, official result, or both players' feelings.
