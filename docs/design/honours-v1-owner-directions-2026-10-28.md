## Consequences and conflicts requiring explicit separation

### Personal milestone, mutable record, and permanent trophy

- **First source-qualified projected Fast Castle**: eligible **Initiate** personal achievement when its fixed approved criterion is satisfied. The owner's tier semantics are decided: **Initiate** first genuine milestone; **Practiced** attainable through diligent improvement but not attendance; **Expert** hard, not universal; **Elite** truly exceptional. A valid milestone is awarded *when earned*, not according to a weekly or Season quota. **No ranks are currently grant-ready** because thresholds and evidence predicates are not approved.
- **Personal best**: owner-only Chronicle ledger with the best currently qualified Game, source/date and correction-safe history; no public numerical leak.
- **Season record**: mutable public best among comparable, source-qualified Games; record holder may change, ties treated according to authoritative comparator, never automatically a trophy.
- **Transferable Fast Castle competitive trophy** (internal ID `AR2_TROPHY_SWIFT_ASCENT`, player-facing name **NOT APPROVED**): a separate **AND** gate of future owner-approved extraordinary absolute projected FC performance and a sufficiently mature Season competitive baseline. A source-qualified record before maturity is provisional and earns no trophy. Once maturity is confirmed, an eligible current record holder claims possession, including when the qualifying Game was recorded earlier; possession starts only when maturity/eligibility is actually established. **Each later strictly better comparable accepted result transfers possession immediately.** Prior holder loses current emblem but remains in valid factual dated succession. Equality does not dethrone an incumbent; a first tied current record with no incumbent needs owner tie policy. Source corrections/withdrawals recompute current best and rightful possession, remove invalidated intervals from legitimate succession and retain audit. Season end archives possession and succession without blocking earlier transfers or creating extra permanent trophies. See [29 Oct policy](honours-v1-milestone-record-policy-197.md) for the three-player challenge case, proposed display names and open numerical gates.
- **Emperor trophy**: its dedicated pinned approved warmup + sole official winning condition stands independent of `The Swift Ascent` maturity; no conversion of a rank-1 Event Roundoff spotlight into a permanent trophy without separately verified award rule.

### Defence, social history, and duplicate merits

The two focused Distinctions are **future concept priorities**, not two present-day award lanes. *Unbroken Line* needs independent severe, actual incoming attack, attributable protective action **and a demonstrable extraordinary defence effect**. *Shield of the Fellowship* needs exact helper→recipient→attacker identity, objectively severe beneficiary danger, executed material aid and an attributable meaningful effect; movement/ORDER near teammate, inferred assists or raw Bond/Chivalry score do not suffice.

Worker D's **The Shield at the Gate** is a separately source-bound extraordinary **social/Chronicle scene**, not a medal. Worker D's proposed **Watchkeeper's Seal** and Worker C's *Shield of the Fellowship* are **colliding candidate merit narratives**: review them together and either reserve just one for that same fact configuration or require demonstrably different criteria. Use canonical **incident keys** and a no-double-medal rule; #188/#199 owns exceptional-scene predicates, Worker C owns permanent-honours taxonomy, Sol/#190 owns synthesis. Neither a Chronicle judgment nor another player's private testimony manufactures an executed rescue or a reverse directed action. Preserve #195 author-private Rivalry/Hostility judgments as context only, with no new direct medal entitlements.

### Visible achievement collection versus private Chronicle

A public family/rank indicator only reflects an **active accepted, explicitly published entitlement**. It must have no direct join or leak to full PB history, exact projected clock, private source/revision, Chronicle author's judgment, pending evidence, or other players' testimony. The player may voluntarily equip one eligible epithet and up to one selected **currently possessed competitive trophy** + one selected independently earned **permanent Distinction** as compact emblems. A transferred trophy must disappear from a former holder's current emblem even though valid factual succession remains. An unfinished/held Distinction has no public earned emblem. Source correction must remove invalid tier/title/emblem; subsequent inferior performance does not remove genuine achievement.
