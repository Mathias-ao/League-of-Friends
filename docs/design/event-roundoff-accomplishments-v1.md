# Event roundoff accomplishments V1

Approved product direction: 8 October 2026. Policy thresholds are deliberate V1 estimates; calibrate from the real league corpus later without rewriting measurement semantics. The source of truth is `functions/src/engines/eventRoundoffCatalogue.ts`.

The roundoff targets **4–5 eligible accomplishments**, in ascending rank order. Higher-ranked eligible accomplishments take precedence over player/category diversity. Only the strongest qualifying tier in each family appears. Related alternatives share a family: Castle/Imperial ages, workforce totals/checkpoints, military queues/checkpoint investment, and Castle/forward placements. One historical accomplishment of each kind can appear; a historical record and its underlying metric cannot double-count the same family. Numerical ties share one card and credit every holder. Sparse evidence yields fewer cards; never fabricate filler.

## All Event Games contribute

The producer supplies every approved Event Match and its `acceptedGameIds` manifest, including series Games. It resolves one current accepted statistics binding per Game. Count every accepted Game once, regardless of recording POV uploads; replace corrected revisions, never add old revisions. Other approved Event Matches contribute even without a scoring act. Cancelled Matches do not contribute. Disputed, ambiguous or missing accepted evidence cannot form a complete Event total.

- Sum raids, Battle/Great Battle participation, applicable team support/tribute, farms, villager/military queues, starting-plus-placed TCs, Castle/forward placements and expansion zones across all Event Games.
- APM is the observed-duration-weighted average across Games, with at least twenty observed minutes in total. Never sum APM or average Game averages without weights.
- For each Game, normalize gold influence by equal share (`100 / starting players`), then duration-weight those ratios. The chosen tier also requires the specified absolute influence in at least one Game. Show the peak percentage in the compact strip and both the index and per-Game percentages in evidence; it does not mean gold mined or verified ownership.
- Pool qualified individual response delays and take the median; at least three responses are required. Never average per-Game medians.
- Age and checkpoint cards inspect every Game, including warm-ups, and keep the best eligible performance. Static age thresholds require a known standard start. Checkpoints require coverage through 20:00.
- Historical records compare individual Game values with already published earlier Games of the exact compatible context and measurement model. Records need an existing baseline, current Season/lifetime inclusion flags, strict improvement and source references. Personal bests require three prior Games and at least ten percent improvement. No historical input means no historical record claim.

Complete accepted Game manifests are mandatory for totals and weighted Event metrics. Missing/incompatible values suppress the affected player's metric, not substitute zero. Team-only metrics exclude inapplicable 1v1s without inventing allied support. Distinct measurement models cannot establish a shared Event leader. Best-performance cards can use available individually qualified performance evidence; the publication coordinator still decides whether the entire Event can release.

## Emperor and result distinctions

Only a designated `WARMUP`, `ONE_V_ONE`, two-player Match against `emperorPlayerIdAtApproval` can qualify. The identity must be pinned before play. Require one sole confirmed winner who is not the Emperor, a positive result revision, and one unambiguous designated warm-up for that Emperor. No current-live Emperor lookup, team-game attribution, FFA defeat or tied victory qualifies. This is roundoff recognition; existing leaderboard bounty rules are unchanged. The producer's warm-up approval snapshot still needs integration.

The unbeaten campaign uses confirmed designated Match outcomes (one warm-up, one main). Winning a series Match does not claim every individual Game was won.

## Display and emblems

The attached design reference is a compact horizontal dark strip with small gold silhouettes, muted serif labels, prominent values and thin vertical separators. The implementation uses four or five equal columns on desktop, two below 850px and one below 540px. Each accomplishment includes a player line, restrained tier label and native expandable evidence section with source Battle links. Evidence remains available to keyboard users and no claim depends on hover.

`web/src/ui/AccomplishmentEmblem.tsx` contains seventeen original SVG silhouettes with a shared aged-gold gradient. Icons cover crowns, laurels, axes, shields, swords, coins, Castles, towers, helmets, wheat, villagers, settlements, banners, compasses, hourglasses, hands and pickaxes. They have no external asset dependency and are decorative (`aria-hidden`); the adjacent text carries meaning. Emblem shapes were rendered and visually inspected separately from the page.

Keep values precise: queues are not completed/surviving units, placements are not completed/surviving buildings, inferred episodes are not kills or confirmed rescues, and infrastructure gold influence is not mining or current territorial ownership. Wonders completed, king kills, actual relic holdings, conversions, destruction and comebacks remain ineligible until independently qualified outcome telemetry exists.

## Integration and validation

`selectEventShowcase` is a pure shared engine for the future Event publication producer. It does not fetch or publish cumulative data, qualify raw recordings, grant achievements, change points or write social progression. The labelled preview invokes it with explicit illustrative opt-in; source Battle statistics use identical snapshots. Production rejects illustrative models/preview hashes by default.

The live backend still does not emit the release/roundoff contract. It must gather manifests, current bindings, pinned warm-up Emperor/start settings and published history, then store the chosen cards in the matching released roundoff revision. The UI continues to require `RELEASED` and an identical positive revision, with no eager Event aggregate request before release.

Tests cover all 64 ranks, every inclusive numerical tier boundary, strict ranking, all-Game totals, multiple series Games, duplicate POVs/reused bindings, source qualification, missing values/models, Emperor restrictions, canonical wins, ties, weighted APM/gold, pooled response medians, best warm-up performance, historical comparisons, family collapse and honest sparse evidence. Desktop/mobile browser inspection remains a separate review task; the environment's preview browser was unavailable.
