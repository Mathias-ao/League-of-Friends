# Age of Friends: what recording evidence can establish for V1b

Read-only investigation, 17 October 2026. Repository main was verified at **`25f120412df5ed0118b9557cdf822ab175108242`**, the merge of PR #120. No production code, authoritative artifacts, commits, PRs, flags, scoring or deployment were changed. The research checkout remained clean.

**Finding: none of the six substantive qualifications is independently executable from the currently decoded and retained evidence examined.** Age of Friends can substantiate recorded commands, source-local chronology, selected object/target references and carefully bounded contextual inferences. These do not yet establish meaningful executed competition, resistance, material assistance, or the required entry circumstances for Gallantry and Cruelty. This is a finding about the present evidence pipeline, not a claim that an AoE2 engine replay could never supply stronger witnesses.

The most useful additional lead is already retained: **FFA tribute observations contain exact donor, recipient, time and nonzero resource components**. They also include many zero-value rows. They are suitable for a narrow proof-of-execution investigation, not immediate Bond or Chivalry qualification.

## 1. Scope, method and evidential limits

The four authentic binaries and their independent TownBell reference reports were read from the acceptance suite's pinned corpus commit, `2d04ec6adbe871d156c108a4351448efeb08f4e6`. ## 3. What survives through the actual evidence chain

Its own retention qualification says initial object search is partial, initial resources are unavailable, semantic completeness is not established, and full-game clock origin is unqualified. It does not retain ongoing unit positions, hit-point changes, accepted attacks, recipient balance changes or a live army ledger. Raw original recordings remain available for a separately validated method; absence from the current schema does not prove impossibility in the binary. ## 5. Evidence gaps and what would actually close them

| Proof element | Already retained but absent from the current social view | Conservatively derivable now | Missing witness / limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meaningful action | Detailed source commands, targets, selection footprints and class uncertainty | Distinct own-player command chains and explicit request type | Executed offensive/defensive activity and independently demonstrable substance; command repetitions do not solve this |
| Entry circumstances | Ordered requests, initial seeds, observation envelope vs strong-start timing | Restrict every candidate context input to a known earlier source moment | Actual meaningful-episode entry and relevant historical state; later result/score cannot fill it |
| Pair-specific attribution | Target instance IDs, earlier controller observations, helper-owned event references | Exact actor; requested recipient; last-observed target association with its age/basis | Live ownership/role when required; positional overlap alone remains insufficient |
| Assistance materiality | Tribute vectors; repair requests; helper/base/threat joins | Nonzero requested resource vector; separately directed request | Accepted transfer/recipient credit or executed material help, with source-linked beneficiary context |
| Defensive counterplay | Defender's own commands and response/garrison-like associations | Separately attributable activity, independent of attacker source rows | Executed resistance rather than receipt/proximity; success or counterattack need not be required |
| Relative strength / sustained disadvantage | Earlier queues, cancellations, requests, catalog labels, command geometry | Requested commitment history and explicit unknowns | Surviving relevant force, completed capabilities, resources/constraints and sustained pre-entry condition; no equivalent witness in current fields |

The replay's currently retained observation schema does not provide continuing simulation state. An authoritative engine playback/state method might provide it from retained original inputs, but that is an **unvalidated additional method**, not hidden information the existing statistics already prove. The existing catalog's projectile/dead-object relationships are reference metadata, not observations of a shot or death. Initial resources are explicitly unavailable; later resource balances cannot be reconstructed soundly just by summing requests.

“Noteworthy,” “material,” and episode significance cannot be replaced with unapproved command-count, army-ratio or duration thresholds. Conversely, kills, victory and successful rescue are not universal requirements: EVID-01 and CAL-03/13 allow substantive unsuccessful effort and defense without counterattack. The missing element is independently demonstrated conduct/context, not compulsory success.

## 6. Specific predicates and the smallest safe sequence

**There is currently no substantive predicate I recommend activating for A–F.** The following two narrow checks are executable today only as factual evidence predicates, not Relationship/Reputation qualification:

| Factual predicate | Required witnesses and direction | Temporal rule | Negative controls, risks and fail-closed behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recorded object-target command with controller history | Same source revision: ORDER by A to instance X; retained initial/previous selection association of X with B; exact event references | Controller observation precedes command by `(atMs, operationOrdinal)`; retain its actual age | Say “target last observed associated with B,” never executed attack/current ownership. Missing/conflicting identity or source reference fails closed. MOVE overlap/shared target does not qualify. C2/C10 are positive command examples. |
| Recorded nonzero directed resource request | DE_TRIBUTE/TRIBUTE actor A, explicit normalized recipient B, finite decoded component vector and source event | Use the command's own time; preserve resources individually and unknown fee/acceptance | All-zero C9 is negative; unknown, malformed or conflicting amount/recipient is unavailable. No reverse direction, receipt, alliance, need or Bond inferred. C8 is positive request evidence. |

These are review proposals, not newly implemented predicates. They introduce no significance threshold. Existing V1a authority/revision/mapping/correction fences would still be prerequisites for any later application read.

Recommended sequence:

1. **First, test the existing tribute witness, without changing upload.** Use C8's 14 nonzero rows and C9's 392 zero rows as the authentic review set. In a separately authorized controlled semantic check, determine whether the recorded components represent an attempted request or accepted execution, including insufficient-resource/rejected cases and tax behavior. Require an independently verified donor/recipient state transition linked to the same operation, or a demonstrably acceptance-only encoding. Two similar balance changes without a causal source link are insufficient. If the format only records requests and no independent state is available, stop: substantive economic assistance remains pending.
2. **Reuse retained fields for review before adding extraction.** Tribute rows already exist in Recorded Events and `economy.tributeSent/tributeReceived`; V1a currently omits those economy fields. A later authorized factual review projection could read them on demand from existing artifacts. It needs no new detector, retained summary or upload-critical-path computation. Statistical `seasonEligibility: team_battles_only` is not a new social policy excluding valid FFA help.
3. **Then attempt executed pair action / defensive action proof.** Require a validated witness of the actor's actual activity and relevant opponent/beneficiary identity, allowing unsuccessful effort and non-counterattacking defense. Reuse source references, episode identity and negative cases C1–C7. Broader extraction or engine-state work is needed only if the retained source cannot provide the witness through a validated method; it requires separate authorization. It must not be smuggled into ordinary upload as an extra detector.
4. **Keep Gallantry, Cruelty and Chivalry pending until their additional contexts are independently proven.** Gallantry needs entry-relative opposition and meaningful daring conduct; Cruelty needs sustained *prior* disadvantage plus later punishing conduct; Chivalry needs material assistance plus independently noteworthy context. Do not reuse general competition qualification as proof of those extra requirements. Keep source facts, substantive receipts and future track interpretation separate.

The smallest safe next step is therefore a **bounded transfer-semantics validation using already retained authentic evidence**, rather than another qualification catalogue or a V1b scoring implementation. If that witness cannot be validated, the concrete result is “no automatic substantive qualification from current evidence,” while useful factual evidence remains available.

## 7. Product choices that genuinely remain

No new product decision is needed to maintain the six pending qualifications. The investigation does not reopen SOC-001–088, REL-V1-EVID-01–06 or REL-V1-CAL-01–17; SOC-089 stays deferred. Formal alliance is not a universal help gate, independent directions remain independent, unsuccessful substantive conduct is allowed, and ordinary combat/victory is insufficient by itself.

If the tribute execution witness is later validated, the bounded product choice is whether **source-proven economic assistance** should be in the first V1b delivery scope. It would still have to satisfy the already approved substantive/materiality requirements. This does not authorize a numerical transfer threshold, subjective intent inference, new player questionnaire or automatic Chivalry. No broader redesign or calibration decision is needed to accept the present findings.
