## Binding interpretation

1. **Provenance, opportunity, aggregation, comparison and prestige are separate gates.** Keep original Game/GUID/source revision, original operation/deed, actor→counterpart and coverage. A missing result does not erase independent source behavior. An unknown value is null/Pending, never zero. A verified result cannot manufacture a replay action.
2. **Game ≠ Battle ≠ Event ≠ Season.** A Battle is the authoritative Match, including all its Games. Select one accepted current source per Game; another POV is not another Game. Normalize each metric correctly within a Battle before equally weighting independent Battles. Historical order is approved Event/MatchPlan/Game order, never upload or completion time. Existing #139 alone applies approved private historical caps, stages, independent directionality and revisions.
3. **Ordinary maps/civilizations/durations belong in longitudinal history.** #204 withdraws identical-map/civ sample gates. Relevant start, format, patch, coverage and opportunity can affect a particular feature/comparator; they do not automatically suppress all five Personality axes or every social track. Two Games in one Battle do not satisfy a two-Battle sample requirement. Keep Developing where actual axis evidence/calibration is insufficient.
4. **The owner's v0.2 gameplay meanings are decided; new methods are not thereby admitted.** Gallantry includes disciplined/effective competition; Cruelty considers economy-directed tactics, with vulnerability a possible amplifier; Chivalry privileges attributable protection, with constructive development requiring its own reviewed method. Rivalry is substantial directed contest, including qualifying defense. Hostility needs meaningful person-specific invasive/asymmetric context, not an ordinary raid. Bond needs actual directed help, not shared team/target. These independent tracks are not zero-sum. Broader meanings do not turn queues into execution, routine booming into morality, or synthetic weights into approved rules.
5. **One underlying incident may have several honest surfaces, no duplicate same-track credit.** Raid/contact/pressure facets retain their original deed lineage. Ordinary Game highlights, Event editorial selection, PB, current record, achievement, exceptional Chronicle scene and permanent honour have independent predicates. A scene or accolade creates no additional conduct Claim. Stages 3+ retain independently evidenced contributions from both actors; strict exceptional Treachery retains all objective gates and binding victim judgment.
6. **Chronicle is source fact plus clearly attributed interpretation.** Every source-current accepted pair Battle deserves a terse log; only genuine development/rare qualified circumstances justify expansion. Stable owner×counterpart×Battle sceneId and seen history survive revisions. A private statement may explain the author's experience, never prove another actor's intention, an effective alliance, a reverse deed or public wrongdoing. Silence/decline never penalizes ordinary progression.
