> **Current approved scope:** Player Reputation = Gallantry/Cruelty/Chivalry, not Treachery. Relationship tracks = Rivalry/Hostility/Bond, separate but directionally evaluated. Every genuine eligible Event Battle, including non-scoring warmups, preserves deeds/provenance and can contribute within Event × pair × track × direction caps; strengthening/weakening capacities are separate. Reciprocity is at most one independently qualified credit per Event/pair/track/direction, may span Events, persists unless corrected, and is independent of capped/displaced ordinary progression. Ordinary stage 3+ needs stage-specific independent reciprocity both ways. Chronology and corrections must be deterministic; missing order blocks only dependent outcomes.
>
> **Exceptional Treachery:** only the strict qualified king-loss alliance-rupture episode, not a Reputation currency. It produces the guaranteed directed victim→responsible-former-ally Hostility stage escalation without creating reciprocity or a mutual Feud. It consumes configurable ordinary Hostility strengthening units inside the same Event cap: Treachery claims take priority, each distinct incident produces a claim, allocated total obeys a hard cap, excess cannot create debt/future deductions, and displaced ordinary credits never carry forward. Claims in each priority class allocate in reliably qualified history order; replay stage effects at original moments. Current exceptional standing does not automatically collapse; later qualified ordinary weakening can regress under the unified framework, including within the same Event.
>
> **Progression:** all three tracks share the structural evaluator, but each has independently qualified signals, thresholds, Event caps and reciprocity/dormancy. Hostility has configurable upward/downward stage-boundary rules, symmetrical offset of unfinished opposite-direction progression, eligible surplus preservation after transitions, potential multiple stage transitions per Event, and independently required reciprocity. Historical peaks, stage transitions and deeds are preserved unless corrected. Never fabricate ordinary points for exceptional stage changes. No numeric rules are approved.
>
> **War Room corrections:** uncertain current Relationship stage suspends new stage-gated actions, but originally valid active challenges may continue while merely pending. Definitively invalid original challenge authorization invalidates active/completed challenge-specific competitive outcomes and points, corrects even closed Season standings and standing-derived titles, preserves independent Battle/League results and original published snapshots, publishes a neutral correction, and directly notifies both affected recipients in-app. Physical prizes already awarded require human organizer review; undelivered physical prizes are held for review subject to obligations. Never automatically reclaim/reassign/financially settle.

> **Most recent decisions:** SOC-054 shared framework; SOC-055 ordinary Rivalry weakening needs rivalry-specific source-backed de-escalation, not merely cooperation or non-contact; SOC-056 permits independently qualified **directional** one-sided Rivalry de-escalation contributions without fake mutual weakening; SOC-057 says an **established stage-3 Rivalry cannot regress below stage 3 until independently qualified acknowledged skill hierarchy exists**.
