# Age of Friends — Contextual Social Deeds V1

Status: approved product direction and qualification/implementation brief; not an implemented scoring engine.
Branch: `feat/pair-social-evidence-v1`.
Foundation: [Pair Social Evidence V1](pair-social-evidence-v1.md).
Authority: [Core Identity](../project/CORE-IDENTITY.md). This document records Mathias's contextual-deed direction and explicit Treachery escalation decision.

## Purpose and boundaries

AoF remembers deeds between people in their actual competitive circumstances. A raid is a triggering episode; its entry circumstances, responses, allied participation and aftermath qualify different interpretations. Reputation is Gallantry, Cruelty and Chivalry. Relationships are Rivalry, Hostility and Bond. Treachery is a turning-point event classification, not a Reputation track.

No Reputation total feeds a Relationship total, or vice versa. The systems may independently interpret the same deed. A league interpretation is not a claim of real-world feeling, motivation, morality or character.

This brief leaves completed statistics, episode boundaries, model versions, schemas and production behavior unchanged. All new context is an additive social view over retained evidence and authoritative league snapshots. Except for the explicitly approved Treachery level rule, numerical points, windows, thresholds, caps and baseline minimums remain unconfigured. No default scoring is activated.

## Evidence and eligibility

Each claim has its own prerequisites. Preserve separately:
- observed command and exact source;
- reconstructed actor/target/controller attribution;
- episode attribution and independence;
- relation-at-event-time qualification;
- contextual measurement and coverage;
- permitted league interpretation.

A high-confidence timestamp does not prove attack success, disadvantage, emotional stress or betrayal. Generic ORDER targeting does not independently prove a weapon attack; action semantics and target attribution remain explicit. Unknown produced-object identity, stale or conflicting control evidence, unsupported patches and missing chronology fail the affected claim closed.

Use claim-specific states: QUALIFIED, UNAVAILABLE, NOT_APPLICABLE and UNCONFIGURED_POLICY. A positive claim may qualify while family completeness remains unavailable. Missing optional context must not discard an otherwise qualified deed; missing required context blocks only its dependent interpretation. No numerical confidence percentage is invented.

## Before, during and after

Before-context is evaluated at the actor's qualified episode-entry moment, never using later score changes. Retain pre-episode score trajectory when independently available, exact leaderboard snapshot, alliance state, and other attributed pressure episodes.

During-context retains each contributor, exact common target, command participation, defensive response, assistance and observed activity windows.

After-context retains subsequent independently qualified episodes and outcomes. It describes chronology; it does not establish causal harm, revenge, surprise, rescue or emotional defeat.

A campaign that makes B weaker does not retroactively earn A a stomping interpretation for its opening raid. A separately qualified later offensive may have different entry circumstances, but ordinary episode continuation does not manufacture another award. Current deed independence marked UNRESOLVED blocks repeated independent contributions.

## Reputation circumstances

Gallantry may qualify meaningful offensive conduct against a comparable or stronger opponent. Cruelty may qualify meaningful punishing pressure against an independently established sustained disadvantage. Chivalry may qualify meaningful protection or assistance. These are independent rules and are not mutually exclusive.

Score is a composite indicator, not surviving military strength. Score differences alone, APM alone, or their uncalibrated combination cannot establish helplessness. Resource commitment estimates are not actual score. Do not train a weakness classifier from fabricated labels or invent percentage certainty.

Evaluate each coalition contributor at their own entry. A may begin an even contest while C joins after B is independently established behind. Neither identical attribution nor identical Reputation follows automatically. No coalition reward is copied to unrelated attackers facing other targets.

Preserve attribution and measurements even when an interpretation is unavailable. Actual damage, elimination and destructive success require qualified outcomes.

## Rivalry and league stakes

Repeated direct competition supplies the history. Pre-Battle standings supply stakes, not motive or playing strength. Store points and rank separately from skill/rating and snapshot the relevant scoring configuration. Two points behind is not itself an award or proof of why a player chose a target.

Standings never create a contest. Any later modifier is bounded and requires a qualifying deed. Gallantry needs its own conduct qualification. Team victory does not prove one player defeated a particular opposing player.

## Activity context

Existing Execution APM and skirmish-window APM retain their exact definitions. Do not relabel them as effective APM or modify those statistics.

The social context adapter may derive deduplicated window measurements from canonical ACTION identities, retaining overall and pair-attributed activity separately. Record window duration, command count, active-second spread, command composition, concurrent episodes, clock basis and source IDs. Observations belonging to several pairs are not evidence of exclusive attention to each.

Define and version windows, comparison populations, minimum samples, baseline method and thresholds before evaluation. Compare similar game phases/formats; use prior evidence available at the relevant historical cutoff. Insufficient history is Developing, not neutral or zero. Recording truncation, restoration, resignation and uncertain participation endpoints block misleading after-context comparisons.

Activity cannot establish maximum effort, stress, emotional collapse or ability to assist. No initial scoring effect. Test whether it adds information beyond detector inputs before considering a modifier; avoid counting the same command intensity twice.

## Treachery: approved exceptional rule

For victim V and responsible former ally T, require:
1. Qualified mutual effective alliance before rupture; preserve V→T and T→V separately.
2. Qualified unilateral rupture by T with full moment ordering.
3. Attributed hostile participation by T after rupture.
4. Confirmed loss of V's king and independently qualified responsibility by T.
5. A configured, versioned association rule linking rupture, participation and elimination. Temporal proximity alone is insufficient.
6. Mode/settings, identity bindings, outcome provenance and policy prerequisites are qualified.

Do not require proof of V's surprise or T's intent. The event records the observable rupture and attributed king elimination. Neutral transitions, an ordinary scheduled opponent, a diplomacy command without qualified effect, or uncertain killer attribution do not qualify.

Let L be V→T Hostility immediately before this distinct event and M the configured maximum (M must be at least 3):
- if L < 3: new level = 3;
- otherwise: new level = min(M, L + 1).

This exceptional directed escalation may occur without reciprocity. It does not manufacture returned hostility or unlock mutual Feud presentation. Ordinary level-three progression retains the core reciprocity requirement; Rivalry and Bond receive no exception.

Apply once per qualified king-loss episode and responsible actor, never once per command or supporting fact. Several cooperating attackers require separately qualified responsibility; participation alone does not name every player a king killer. Multiple genuine events apply in stable chronology. Do not apply an additional generic king-loss escalation to the same event unless a later explicitly approved policy defines stacking. Missing stage/max configuration leaves evaluation UNCONFIGURED_POLICY.

Historical Bond may be damaged under a separate explicit rule using pre-Battle state. No point amount or automatic Reputation award is set here. Treachery is a priority Chronicle event after qualification, even when other unrelated rule families remain unconfigured.

## Episode accounting, chronology and corrections

One deed retains many facets. Raid, response, elevated activity and battle promotion are not four independent acts. Support-under-pressure enriches existing assistance; it does not merge neighboring reinforcement and defence without qualified continuity. Return raids and later assistance retain their independent source deeds.

Create one ordered chapter per pair per league Battle with Game provenance. Technical engagement “Battle” means an inferred combat episode and is not the league Battle. Cross-Game ordering uses approved Game sequence, then full replay moment; no invented chronology for missing order.

Store replay/canonical revision, source models, league identity mapping revision, standings/event/rule snapshots, baseline definition and actual baseline source revisions. Identity is content-based and input-order independent. Corrected source or standings revisions invalidate dependent chapters and replace contributions; never append both old and new. Baselines affected by corrections require explicit dependent rebuilds.

Chronicle entries retain factual sources, eligible claims and unavailable claims. “Return raid” is ordered history, not revenge. “Repair orders” is not completed rescue. “Activity declined” is not surrender unless surrender is separately recorded. “Treachery” is the qualified league event, not a character judgment.

## Implementation slices and acceptance

1. Pure additive episode-context projection: existing pressure/response/support joins, independent return raids and exact coalition membership. Missing chronology blocks ordered claims only. No persistence/scoring/statistics changes.
2. Ordered league pair chapters and authoritative pre-Battle standings: durable identity bindings, Game order, source revisions and correction behavior; no guessed context.
3. Activity annotations: prior comparable baselines, attribution and coverage. No progression effect; evaluate against retained real examples and unrelated/concurrent controls.
4. Controlled qualification: tribute and repair/guard commands; effective initial/runtime diplomacy. Preserve command-only observations until each gate passes.
5. Independent outcomes: qualified score trajectories, king loss/responsibility and objective possession/taking. Retain permanently sufficient canonical evidence before temporary recordings are deleted; no player-entered post-match statistics.
6. Explicit rule configuration and shadow evaluation, then separately scoped production integration after prerequisites and representative review pass.

Required cases include: even contest becoming one-sided; pre-existing disadvantage before entry; later coalition entrant; A/B→X and B/C→Y; response without returned targeting; independent return raid; reverse support in another form; unrelated APM spike; declining activity at recording end; same-millisecond rupture ordering; command-only diplomacy; unknown killer; ordinary king elimination; one-sided Treachery below/at/above level 3; maximum cap; duplicate event copies; corrected attribution; multiple Games; unavailable versus no interaction.

Inspect representative real ledger chapters and retain qualification failures as diagnostics. Verify the existing statistics are byte-for-byte unchanged for the same evidence/schema snapshot by additive context work, excluding only the separately emitted social artifact. Run meaningful unit checks for the new interpreter when implemented, plus existing applicable CI. This documentation change itself does not certify new implementation or new test results.
