## Source method admission is not source authority or a social credit

The new `admit_contextual_source_grades(statistics,context,source_measurements,action_events,game_scope,policy,producer)` first *rebuilds* the accepted #158 producer from its actual original canonical action events, Pair deeds, statistics and Context V2; a supplied producer must match the entire regenerated document. Its output keeps `sourceCoverage=LIMITED` for all original #149 observations. Only **exact** listed family+methodVersion with independently satisfied method requirements can obtain separate `methodCoverage=ADMITTED`, `decision=SOURCE_ADMITTED`, and private `gradeBandId=ORDINARY`. No generic coverage upgrade, no inferential credit from mere source count.

This means **two distinct originally actor-owned command event IDs are necessary but never sufficient**. We additionally require an independently derived, opponent-specific detector tied to those original events and a second distinct contextual form, with original `(recordingAtMs,operationOrdinal)` complete.

**Cross-component anti-farming:** #158 deduplicates within a connected original deed/incident component. A long Raid V3 or promoted skirmish may independently overlap **multiple** such components with disjoint event memberships. #167 extends dedup for each exact actor→counterpart→track across source components: if the later component reuses a Raid/Skirmish/Battle/Help detector already admitted for that direction/track in the same Game, its grade remains **PENDING** with `CORRELATED_DETECTOR_EPISODE_ALREADY_ADMITTED`. It retains its source refs for audit, but does not mint another method-admitted episode. Distinct opposite-direction conduct stays separate; different tracks may independently qualify under their own predicates. This is conservative until owner review permits separable subepisodes. The observed command+corroborating detectors support bounded inference of deliberate directed *command commitment*; they do **not** prove damage, kills, actual help delivered, rescue, risk motive, alliance rupture, verified pre-existing weakness, or intent.

The pilot policy `pilot_policy(min_original_commands=2,max_preentry_ms=90000)` is pure and adjustable within hard floors (2–20 distinct original commands and 1,000–180,000ms past-only lookback). It remains `PROPOSED_EMULATOR_ONLY`, `approvalRef=null`. A client cannot select a new method registry, reduce source floor below two, set SUBSTANTIAL, insert approved significance bands, or bypass the scope by supplying an older producer. `SUBSTANTIAL` is **not admitted** until separate materiality methodology. `ORDINARY` is a provisional source-bound grade, not a numerical historical weight approved for release.
