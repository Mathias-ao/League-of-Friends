# AoF V1 ordinary inference amendment

17 October 2026 · `AOF_SOCIAL_V1_SHARED_1` · [contract](../../contracts/social-v1/README.md)

The [Organiser 2 review](https://github.com/Mathias-ao/League-of-Friends/issues/122#issuecomment-6240004525) accepts the technical baseline for parameterized, inactive implementation. The owner has decided inference-first ordinary V1, five automatic Personality sliders, six independently inferred social tracks with meaningful strengthening and weakening, rare optional Chronicle contributions, trophies, exceptional events and low-cost storytelling. The replay-engine observer is **post-V1**, with no launch dependency.

This amendment supersedes older ordinary execution-proof and reporting-scope restrictions only as specified below. It does not approve detailed predicates, scoring values, new Hostility paths, numerical balancing or exceptional privileges. Original research findings remain unchanged. No scoring defaults, publication wiring or activation are introduced by the shared contract.

## Narrow amendment


**Amendment ID: AOF-SOC-V1-INFERENCE-01. Owner-directed inference method; detailed rule wording and predicate/calibration packs remain proposed.** Applies only to ordinary V1 progression and its ordinary opportunity/milestone admission; not strict exceptional proofs.

> Ordinary V1 Reputation and directional Relationship effects may qualify from calibrated, source-backed, bounded inference of meaningful conduct. Each admitted family must combine relevant behavioral and attribution/context evidence, specify coverage, alternatives and conservative limits, and pass reviewed authentic examples and adversarial controls. An isolated ambiguous command, raw command frequency, detector label, shared result, co-presence or shared target is insufficient. Correlated facets from the same underlying commands are not independent corroboration.
>
> An inferred qualification authorizes a versioned league interpretation, not a declaration that an attack landed, resources were received, a building completed, a rescue occurred, or a motive existed. Every claim, receipt and narrative retains this distinction. Higher sample volume cannot erase semantic uncertainty.
>
> Required episode-entry circumstances may be represented by explicitly calibrated pre-entry proxies where direct state is unavailable. Preserve their proxy names and limitations: requested military commitment is not surviving army strength. Do not use later outcomes, current standings or post-entry changes to manufacture earlier circumstances. Missing required attribution, chronology, opportunity or context leaves the affected claim Pending or Unconfigured, never a guessed zero or a positive.
>
> Strengthening and weakening require separate approved predicates. One incident may independently support several effects, with overlap deduplication and separate budgets; there is no automatic cross-track conversion or zero-sum transfer. Inactivity, missing evidence and receipt of another player's action do not manufacture counter-conduct.
>
> Ordinary reciprocity requires each player's own independently attributable source-backed conduct, separately admitted under the approved reciprocity predicate. Target/victim status, received assistance, team assignment, copied inference, a received opinion or a mirror of the other direction cannot establish it. Event limits, earliest reliable anchors and stage-specific ordinary 3+ gates remain unchanged.
>
> Strict Treachery, confirmed objective accomplishments and other explicitly proof-bound exceptions retain their original evidence requirements. No ordinary inference can satisfy their missing proof. The replay-engine observer is not a V1 dependency; unavailable strict features remain unavailable without blocking ordinary inference or creating fictional accomplishments.
