## Exact inputs and output

`project_social_source_measurements(statistics, context, action_events, lookback_ms)` uses the existing immutable source-revision envelope, Pair Social Evidence V1 source deed, Worker 1 Context V1/V2, and **original retained** canonical Action Events (including recorded actor, eventId, timestampMs, operationOrdinal). The producer indexes original events, rejects conflicting event IDs and stale source envelopes, refuses a forged directional actor, and bounds explicit lookback and event capacity.

Per-episode observation identity is derived from source replay hash, context revision, original source deed ID, source actor and counterpart, family and exact original source refs. A source correction/replacement changes the revision, rather than adding historical points. There is no time/name-based source-deed remap. Multiple distinct Games with the same scoped Game document ID are kept separate **by the trusted external source binding and #139**, not by this Game-local producer. Repeated POVs of one GUID are not independent history.

**All private producer output is LIMITED, qualifiesSocialFact=false**. A decoded ORDER/BUILD/queue/tribute is a request, not execution; an inferred pair-directed raid or assist detector is a candidate, not a confirmed encounter, rescue, pressure damage or resource benefit. Past requested command volume is explicitly NOT surviving army strength, victim disadvantage or challenge. Missing source/context evidence gives no counterfactual proof. If there are no candidate observations, it is not evidence of peaceful play.

## Proposed independent weakening pack — requires explicit owner approval

**These are candidate meaning gates, not coded effects and not automatically accepted from missing actions.**

| Track | Independently positive weakening evidence required | Strict counterexample |
|---|---|---|
| Gallantry | Qualified deliberate abandonment of a personally chosen, materially challenging commitment **while a recorded genuine opportunity and capacity remain**; own withdrawal and contemporaneous counterpart threat/availability must be corroborated | Normal tactical retreat, recovery, losing army, missing commands, or an inferior result |
| Cruelty | Meaningful, attributable reparative relief **to the specific prior victim**, independently verified as material and occurring after prior qualified Cruelty conduct; history and chronology gate mandatory | Generic charity, team switch, no further attacks, later absence, ordinary enemy peace |
| Chivalry | Independently substantiated knowing withdrawal of a specific protective commitment **while helper capacity, recipient need, knowledge and reachable opportunity are established** | No visible assist, failure to reach ally, ordinary loss, missing beneficiary |
| Rivalry | Actor-owned, intentional resolution/de-escalation evidence tied to an existing pair rivalry, with contemporaneous opportunity or explicit structured acknowledgment; #139 protected floor remains authoritative | No matches played, mere team alignment, passive survival, unexplained inactivity |
| Hostility | Meaningful directed aid to former adversary **plus independent source-backed reconciliation context**, separately qualified from Bond | Shared target, alliance flag alone, generic teammate commands, silence |
| Bond | Serious, unusually antagonistic, directed act **against a specifically established beneficiary/cooperator**, with independent prior cooperation and ordered rupture/harm context | Ordinary combat after game start, opponent victory, a mere diplomacy request |

A single qualified incident may strengthen one track and weaken another **only under two distinct versioned predicates**, each with its own provenance and approved budget. None of these meanings may be inferred from missing data; current authentic observations are insufficient for the three Reputation weakening predicates. Hostility cannot arise from routine military confrontation; Bond cannot arise from copied reciprocal assists or shared target. Strict Treachery remains the separate proof/binding-victim route; its preexception weakening disposition stays PENDING as defined by merged #139.

## Needed producer increments (separately reviewable, method-specific)

1. **Challenge and victim-entry condition:** restrict to *past-only* queue/research/eco requested-commitment windows with actor and opponent source refs, explicit relevant catalog class and known match/economy coverage. Output a measurement of **requested investment**, never live army/wealth, with independent opportunity/counterevidence; require owner-calibrated how to compare two parties and thresholds before claiming Gallantry/Cruelty.
2. **Directed help / threatened recipient:** combine original helper-owned interventions, independently prior recorded victim-threat candidates and stable aid recipient. Where normalized tribute exists, preserve donor/recipient, component vector, zero/nonzero distinction and source ID; separate **request** from actual receipt, and require an independently justified materiality rule.
3. **Cooperation/rupture + hostility:** retain actual diplomacy-command actor, requested new state, prior requested state and associated directed episode, **without asserting effective runtime allegiance**. Do not infer distinct antagonism unless effective history and independently significant post-rupture pressure are measured. FFA team sentinel must be corrected upstream before a positive/negative based on effective topology.
4. **Authentic negative holdouts and lineage:** paired POV remap, corrections/withdrawals/source revisions, multiple Battles containing `game-1`, one event cited under two different labels, same-moment conflicting ordinal and independent actor conduct. Reject any reupload aggregation without active source arbitration.
